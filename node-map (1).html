Who is working near this

Sixty-five people, groups, doctrines and literatures sitting around the claim that a professional entry requirement can be assessed as valid or invalid. Six clusters — technical standards, disability studies, law, measurement, programme design, and fidelity — with the original contribution at the centre. Circle size is proximity to that contribution, not eminence. The largest circles are the ones that could most easily absorb, pre-empt, or co-author it.

Every node lists the works to look up. Drag to move, scroll to zoom, and use By affinity to see which clusters actually touch — the honest finding of this map is which two do not.

How to read this. By cluster groups people by the literature they publish in. By affinity repositions the same nodes by how alike they actually are — shared works and shared themes — which is where the map earns its keep: the measurement cluster drifts away from everything else, because nobody in the standards literature cites it and nobody in it cites the standards literature. That separation is the gap.

On verification. Nodes marked checked against a live source have at least one link that was retrieved and read while this map was built. Everything else comes from background knowledge and was not re-verified — affiliations, titles and publication details change, so treat those as leads to confirm rather than settled facts. Anyone can be missing; absence here is not evidence of absence in the field.
