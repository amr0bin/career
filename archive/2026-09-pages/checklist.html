What I need to do, at every stage,
to be ready to act.

This checklist tracks readiness at each phase of the career and education path — not just credential completion, but the concrete conditions that allow me to deploy, create value, and move with purpose. Each item is a gate I need to pass, not just a box to tick.

The principle underneath all of this
Readiness is not a credential. It is the combination of knowledge, relationships, evidence, and positioning that allows me to act with authority in the next context. Each phase of this checklist asks the same question: not "have I completed the requirement?" but "am I genuinely prepared to contribute?" Those are different questions, and only the second one matters.

Foundation — The First Publication
Write and Submit the Conceptual Paper

This is the single highest-leverage action available to me right now. A published paper — or even a submitted one — changes every application and conversation that follows it.

Highest priority

Three months to draft · The argument that only my specific combination of expertise can make

The argument is specific: designing the learning systems that train practitioners to deliver depression interventions is as important as designing the interventions themselves — and accessibility must be built into both from the beginning, not retrofitted at the end. This is the paper that introduces me to the field as a thinker, not just a practitioner.

  • Complete the foundational reading list first — the argument needs to sit credibly within the existing literature

The paper does not need to be perfect before submission. It needs to make a real argument that the field has not made. Reviewers will improve it. The act of submitting is the move.
