Living document

Six streams, five stages, one page.

The plan has never felt fragmented because it is fragmented. It feels that way because it has been written down six times, one stream at a time, and never once laid side by side. This is the side-by-side view: what is running at full rhythm in each stage, what is deliberately at its floor, what has not started, and what pays for it.

Follows career.html as the spine. Where the earlier sequence documents disagree with it, this page follows career.html, because it is the later and more decided of the two. Both were revised in September 2026 to replace the counselling psychology doctorate with an art therapy diploma and an art doctorate; the-sequence.html was revised at the same time. Nothing here is keyed to a date except the four things that genuinely are.

What pays for itThe income in play

Salary plus consulting. The accumulation begins. Size the three troughs on one page of arithmetic now, years early.

Salary plus consulting, at peak. Fees are payable module by module. This is where the buffer is built or it is not built at all.

First trough. Staff tuition benefit if the employer is the school, plus the buffer, plus consulting turned flexible, plus product income.

Second trough, short. Diploma fees and placement hours against a reduced salary and the retainer. The first practice income arrives at the end of it.

Third trough, the long one. Funding package, assistantships, product income, a small caseload, whatever fraction of the salary survives. Entered funded or not entered.

Five uncorrelated streams. Salary, private practice, teaching, licensed materials, the studio's own income. No single one can coerce a decision.

Stage by stage

What each stage actually asks for

Each stage states what it requires to enter, what runs inside it, and the single condition that closes it. The streams are listed in the order they consume attention in that stage, not in a fixed order — which is itself information.

Stage one

Ground

Put the existing work on the record, get inside a university, and answer the question the whole path rests on.

Requires nothing. This stage is underway. It contains no enrolment, which is what gives the publishing, the job search, and the crisis-line training room to happen at all.

Service — the Crisis Centre applicationPeak+

This goes first in the stage, not because it is the largest item but because it is the one with a queue in front of it. Cohorts are limited, intake windows are fixed, and applying does not guarantee a place.

  • Roughly 110 hours of basic training plus 32 supervised hours, then 250 credited service hours at a mandatory four a week, including overnight and fringe shifts
  • Volunteers pay for basic training themselves — a real cost, and one to confirm before committing
  • Accredited by the American Association of Suicidology, and recognised across BC as relevant experience for counselling psychology admission
  • A reference letter follows completion

What it actually buys is not the letter. It is a direct answer, years earlier than the sequence documents allowed for, to the question every later stage assumes: is sitting with one person in acute distress, in real time, work I want. Make Space is group facilitation and does not answer it.

Salaried work — move inside a BC universityFull+

Accessibility services, student services, or learning design at UBC or SFU — now specifically these two, because the counselling master's and the doctorate are both meant to be taken from inside the employer. The institution matters more than the title. It places the role inside the institution the path ends in, among the people who will later write references, supervise practicum, and sit on hiring committees.

There is a second reason, and it belongs in the financial plan rather than the career one: staff status at a BC university is the single most plausible route to subsidised tuition and to a flexible arrangement during the counselling master's. Both need confirming with the specific employer, and both are worth confirming before choosing between offers.

Practice & publishing — get the work on the recordPeak+
  • Outcome measurement in Make Space. A validated depression screen at the start and end of each cycle. The smallest change with the largest downstream effect — it turns a practice into a programme with a defensible claim, and it turns a curriculum into something licensable
  • The four accessibility resources, published. All written, none public. Revise the essential-requirements piece first; its scarcity claim predates the AFMC technical standards reform
  • The conceptual paper, submitted. No affiliation required, and it changes the tenor of every application and conversation that follows
Study — CPACC and the paperwork that has a deadline behind itReduced+
  • CPACC. Self-paced, inexpensive, and the only credential in the plan that raises earning power inside the current job search. Every month it is deferred is a month of foregone rate
  • A Visual Communication Design certificate, part-time. It pays in the workbooks and the advisor role at once, and it starts the design portfolio that one of the three doctoral doors reads
  • The prerequisite inventory. Check the earlier UBC coursework against the department's published equivalency table before enrolling in anything, since equivalency is no longer adjudicated in advance
  • The Leeds application. Two intakes, March and September 2027
Consulting & products — start the engine that earns without youFull+

Two moves, and the second is the one the portfolio has described for years without building.

  • Price the consulting as a specialism, not a service. The scarcity is the intersection — disability studies, accommodation law, essential requirements, and learning design held together — not any one component. It takes an afternoon to rewrite and it changes the rate
  • Ship the first Groundwork workbook. Small enough to finish in weeks, deliberately incomplete, because the branches are what it advertises. Its return this quarter is credibility and an email list, not cash — but the stream needs eighteen to twenty-four months to become meaningful, which is exactly why it starts here and not in the trough
Studio — one piece a quarterFloor+

Dear Ordinary at its own pace, protected as firmly as any deadline. It is the embodied knowing the analytical work cannot generate alone, and the mechanism by which this many modes are held at once and kept in relation to each other.

What pays — and the one page of arithmetic that is overdueAccumulation+

Salary plus consulting, at the highest earning capacity relative to demands anywhere on the path. Three financial moves belong here rather than later:

  • Size all three troughs. Tuition, plus the earnings not made, plus the number of years of it, for Stages three, four and five separately. One page. It is years early, and that is the point
  • Start the buffer now, not when Stage three appears on the horizon
  • Confirm the employer's tuition benefit and flexible-hours provisions while there is still a choice of employer

Complete when

The CPACC is held, the four resources are live, the paper is submitted, one Make Space cohort has pre- and post-data, the working role is inside a post-secondary institution, and Crisis Centre training is complete with shifts underway. The 250 service hours continue into Stage two; the training is what closes this one.

Stage two

Leeds

The only degree in this path that can be held alongside a salary — which makes it the only stage where the buffer can be built.

Requires a bachelor's degree with relevant experience. Fifteen years of applied accessibility practice clears the stated route comfortably. Applications are individually assessed and may include an interview.

Study — the MSc and the prerequisites, at the same timePeak+

MSc Disability Studies, Rights and Inclusion — University of Leeds, online, part-time.

  • Nine taught modules of fifteen credits, eight weeks each. Roughly ten hours of guided learning and ten hours of independent study per week while a module is running — the honest weekly cost, and the reason every other stream drops to its floor during module weeks
  • The forty-five-credit applied project. This is where the essential-requirements corpus study gets written, supervised, credited and finished. Doing it inside a programme with a deadline is the difference between a study that exists and one that is always about to begin
  • The five undergraduate prerequisites alongside: interviewing skills, counselling theories, learning or cognitive psychology, abnormal psychology, and statistics. Evening and online
  • The supervisor conversation. Admission to the research-thesis master's depends on supervisory expertise being available in the declared research area, and disability is named among the department's core research foci
Service — the 250 hours land inside this stageFull+

Four hours a week of crisis line and text support, with skills reviews at three and nine months, running through the same months as the Leeds modules. This is the first real collision in the plan and it has a designed answer: Leeds permits breaks between modules and allows up to four years to complete. Taking fewer modules during the year the service hours are accumulating is a use of the programme's design, not a lapse in the plan.

The reference letter and the completed hours need to be in hand well before the counselling master's application is written, which is the actual deadline governing this stream.

Salaried work — held at full time, on purposeFull+

The placement of Leeds here is structural rather than sentimental. Every later stage requires daytime hours: the counselling master's and the art therapy diploma both carry placements, and the doctorate may require full-time study. This is the only window in the whole path where a graduate degree and a full-time salary can be assumed to coexist. Taken later, it cannot be taken at all.

The financial discipline that follows is a single refusal: coursework does not justify a reduction in earnings that the programme does not require.

Consulting & products — hold the clients, ship the branchesReduced+

The practice holds existing clients and does not take new scope. What does grow is the part that does not cost hours later:

  • Branch workbooks on a cadence. The accessibility branch has a different buyer from the rest — an organisation with a training line item, and no comparable product on the market. Do not let the first price set the second
  • One retainer, secured before Stage three. A single long-running relationship at a capped monthly scope is worth more in a trough than three project clients, because it survives a daytime placement schedule
Practice & publishing — the weekly session and the reflective noteReduced+

Make Space continues at one session weekly, with a brief reflective note after each one. That note is the whole discipline of this stage: three years of them become a qualitative paper that needs no doctoral affiliation. The public writing drops to its floor of one substantial piece monthly and stays there.

Studio — unchangedFloor+

One piece per quarter. The floor does not move during the busiest stage; that is what makes it a floor rather than a preference.

What pays — the accumulation windowPeak earning+

Full salary plus consulting, against fees payable module by module — around £1,170 per taught module and £2,220 for the project on the currently published figures, which turns a degree into a series of affordable decisions rather than one large one.

Stated plainly, because nothing else in the plan says it: almost every dollar that carries Stages three and four is earned in Stage one and Stage two. There is no later window. That reframes Leeds as a financial stage as much as an academic one, and it is the strongest argument for pushing rate hard right now.

Complete when

The applied project is submitted, all five prerequisites are held with transcripts in hand, at least one supervisor whose work touches disability has agreed to a conversation, and the 250 service hours are complete with the reference letter received.

Stage three

The counselling master's

Clinical formation, the registration that opens the room, and the first stage where the salary cannot hold its current shape.

Requires the five prerequisites, a B+ senior average, a criminal record clearance before the supervised clinical placement, and — for the thesis route — an available supervisor in the declared research area. The MA or the MEd: with no counselling doctorate downstream, the thesis is no longer what the next application is read for. The rule is now to choose the programme the salary can survive, and take the thesis only if it does not cost a year.

Study — coursework, practicum, and the choice of schoolFull+
  • Forty-eight credits of coursework, with a six-credit thesis if the MA is chosen. Recent graduates average three years
  • UBC or SFU first — a staff member studying at their own institution is the cheapest version of this stage there is. If a full-time role and the programme cannot be held together at either, two alternates are already named: Adler University's Master of Counselling Psychology in Vancouver, and Athabasca's online Master of Counselling. Both checked before the UBC and SFU answer is assumed
  • If there is a thesis, it treats the disability and depression intersection — and doubles as the first research document an art doctorate committee will read
  • Practicum placements requested in post-secondary counselling and disability services, so the clinical hours accrue in the setting the career stays in
  • Registration as a clinical counsellor applied for on conferral

One scheduling fact belongs in the plan rather than in the surprise: the supervised clinical course runs in the second year and requires one day a week away from work.

Salaried work — the shape changes hereReduced+

A senior full-time role cannot be carried alongside daytime practicum placements, and planning as though it can is what puts every later stage under strain. The arrangement is made at the start of the stage, alongside the financial plan that carries it, not discovered inside it.

Four arrangements, in rough order of how much they preserve:

  • Reduced hours with the same employer — the whole reason the Stage one target is now UBC or SFU specifically, and why staff study provisions decide between offers
  • A secondment or internal move to a role with daytime flexibility
  • Contract work replacing employment, priced at specialism rate to make fewer hours viable
  • A leave, funded from the buffer, for the second year only — or, if none of these hold, the online alternate instead of the campus programme
Consulting & products — now the flexible income, not the side projectFull+

This is the stage the retainer and the workbook line were built for. Consulting becomes the income that bends around a placement schedule, and the products are the part that keeps earning during weeks with no capacity at all. Neither can be started here — a stream begun in a trough arrives too late to help with it.

Practice & publishing — at the floorFloor+

One Make Space session weekly, one substantial piece of writing monthly. Clinical training comes first in this stage, and saying so in advance is what stops it being negotiated week by week.

Service — completeComplete+

The crisis line hours finished in Stage two, and its three products — the reference letter, the supervised distress experience on the application, and a first-hand answer to whether this work is wanted — are all in hand before this stage begins. Nothing is owed to it here.

Studio — Sundays kept, shown quarterlyReduced+

The studio-day standing order holds where it can, and the catalogue never lapses even when a Sunday does. This is no longer a quarterly floor. Two stages from here a doctoral committee reads this record, and it cannot be assembled retroactively.

What pays — the first troughTrough+

Reduced salary, plus the staff tuition benefit if the employer is the school, plus the buffer, plus consulting turned flexible, plus product income. Five sources, none of them sufficient alone, which is the design.

There is also a legitimate lever the portfolio should say out loud: the trough can be made shallower by taking this stage more slowly, part-time where the programme permits, at the cost of years. Time is the cheapest currency this plan has, and the plan is keyed to conditions rather than dates precisely so that this trade is available.

Complete when

The degree is conferred, the practicum hours are complete, and the RCC application is submitted.

Stage five

The doctorate

The terminal degree for the art lectern, on the studio's own terms — and the longest financial exposure in the path.

Requires the master's, a supervisor who wants the work, a catalogued and shown body of studio work, a publication record — and funding, a condition of equal standing to the other four. SFU's PhD in Contemporary Arts opens applications on 1 October for the following September; UBC's PhD in Art History is the scholarly alternative, and whether it admits on a counselling master's plus a BFA is unverified.

Study — the thesis is the studioPeak+
  • At SFU: a minimum of thirty-five units, qualifying examinations, a thesis prospectus, and a practice-based or scholarly thesis, in twelve to fifteen semesters. A supervisor is matched on admission
  • The practice-based thesis culminates in a major original artwork or public presentation alongside the written component — Dear Ordinary's question, held inside the scholarly frame the rest of the path built
  • Teaching assistantships from the first year, because they are both the funding and the teaching record the adjunct appointment is later assessed on

Whether the programme can be held alongside a university role, or requires the salary to stop, is the first thing to establish. Every financial arrangement in this stage follows from the answer.

What pays — enter funded, or do not enterDeep trough+

An unfunded doctorate here does not merely cost tuition. It reduces or switches off the salary during what would otherwise be peak earning years, for a credential that has a stated floor beneath it — two registrations, the accessibility role, and sessional studio teaching still deliver most of the life without it. Funded, this is an excellent decision. Unfunded, it is the most expensive item in the plan, and the honest answer may be no.

Five sources to have confirmed and, where possible, secured before the application, not after the offer:

  • The school's funding package and multi-year fellowships — SFU's and UBC's, separately, because they differ
  • Tri-council awards — SSHRC, which is where a research-creation doctorate sits — and provincial graduate scholarships
  • Teaching and research assistantships, which also build the teaching record
  • Employer support or tuition benefit, if the Stage one role is still held in some form
  • The practice, held small, and the product income that does not need hours
Salaried work — at whatever fraction the programme permitsReduced+

Employment in its earlier form may end here, or may continue at a fraction. Unlike the counselling doctorate this replaced, the answer is not written into the programme description and has to be asked. If the answer is none, assistantships are the closest equivalent and they are part of the programme rather than alongside it.

Product income continues without costing hours, which is the entire reason it was built. Consulting is hard-capped — the temptation in this stage is to take the project that carries the year and lose the year to it. A named monthly ceiling, set at the start, is easier to hold than a decision made inside the stage.

Practice & publishing — the caseload held smallFloor+

Therapeutic Studio runs at a handful of hours a week — enough to keep both registrations current and the practice alive, not enough to compete with the thesis. Make Space runs at its floor or, better, is handed to a trained facilitator, which is also the first real test of whether the method transfers. Studio — this is the stagePeak+

Four to five years of making inside a programme, with a supervisor, a cohort, studio space, and an examination at the end that is an exhibition. Every earlier version of this plan held the studio at a floor for twenty years in order to expand it at the end. This is the expansion, and it arrives a decade sooner.

The part that was missing

What pays for the years that will not pay for themselves

There are three stages in this path that a salary cannot carry at full strength, and they are known in advance. The previous version of this page had two; the art therapy diploma adds a short third between them. Everything financial in the plan is about arriving at them prepared rather than discovering them from inside.

The three troughs, named

Stage three · shallow, long

One day a week away from work in the second year, plus practicum placements in daytime hours. Income drops rather than stops. It can be made shallower by taking the degree more slowly, if the programme permits — which is itself a thing to confirm.

Stage four · short, shallow

Eighteen months of diploma fees and a 600-hour placement in daytime hours, against a reduced salary and the retainer. The counselling registration from the stage before permits paid practice before this one ends, which is what keeps it shallow.

Stage five · long, and optional

Four to five years in a doctoral programme whose part-time provisions are unverified. Income drops, or stops in its earlier form, for the longest stretch on the path. This one has a floor beneath it: the plan still delivers the university post, the practice at both registrations, and sessional studio teaching without it.

Four things can carry a trough. They are not equally reliable.

The buffer

Entirely within your control, and the only source that cannot be withdrawn by someone else. It requires two numbers that do not currently exist in any document in this repository: the annual cost of the life actually wanted, and the size of each trough. That page of arithmetic is the highest-value thing available this month and it does not wait on anything.

The buffer is built in Stage one and Stage two or it is not built. There is no later window, because every stage after Stage two reduces income by design — and there are now three of them before the portfolio, not two.

Institutional money

The most under-explored source in the plan, and the reason Stage one's target is a role inside a BC university rather than merely inside health. Staff tuition benefits, internal flexible-hours provisions, assistantships, departmental funding packages, and scholarship eligibility all sit here. With the doctorate now in an art school rather than a counselling department, the internship stipend is gone from this list and SSHRC arrives on it.

None of it is confirmed. All of it is checkable now, and some of it should influence which offer is accepted in Stage one — which is the only reason it appears this early on the page.

Income that does not need hours

The Groundwork workbooks, licensed training materials, a course, eventually the manualised facilitator curriculum. This is the only kind of income that survives a stage with no spare capacity in it, and it is the mechanism by which a therapist with a deliberately small caseload is nonetheless secure.

Its constraint is time-to-maturity. A product line takes eighteen to twenty-four months to earn anything meaningful, which means it must be started in Stage one to be useful in Stage three, and cannot be started in a trough to solve one. It now has to carry three troughs, which is a stronger argument for starting it, not a weaker one.

Retained consulting at a floor

One or two long-running relationships at a capped monthly scope, priced as a specialism rather than a service. More valuable in a trough than several project clients, because a retainer survives an unpredictable placement schedule and a project does not.

The risk it carries is the opposite one: consulting expands to fill whatever room it is given, and the stage most in need of income is the stage least able to afford the hours. Hence the cap, set in advance.

What to build now, in order

Multiple streams is the right instinct and building five at once during a job search is how it fails. Three things, in this sequence, each started only when the one before it is running:

  • One product line. Groundwork, foundation workbook first, small enough to ship in weeks
  • One retained relationship. Named scope, monthly, at specialism rate
  • One buffer, with a floor that is not spent whatever the opportunity

Everything else — the caseload, teaching income, the book — arrives on its own schedule and none of it needs to be engineered now.

The rule that makes the rest of it work

No stage that reduces income is entered before it has been sized and funded. Applied to Stage five this is stricter still: funding is a condition of entry with the same standing as the supervisor and the portfolio. Not a hope attached to a decision already made.

This page describes structure, not products. Any decision about accounts, tax treatment, or investments belongs with an accountant or fee-only planner familiar with the BC picture.

Where the streams actually conflict

Five collisions, and what gives

Fragmentation is not caused by the number of streams. It is caused by not having decided in advance which one yields when two of them want the same week. These are the five places on the path where that happens, each with the answer written down before it is needed.

Crisis line training against a live Leeds module

110 hours of training plus 32 supervised hours does not fit beside 20 hours a week of module study and a full-time role. What gives: the training finishes in Stage one and the shifts are steady before a first module starts. On current shape that points to the September 2027 Leeds intake rather than March — six months of established shifts is a much better footing than starting both in the same month.

The prerequisite deadline against the module schedule

The five prerequisites must be complete by 31 December in the year of application; the Leeds modules have no such date and the programme allows up to four years. What gives: the modules. Anything with a fixed external date outranks anything without one, every time.

The 250 service hours against module weeks

Four hours a week, mandatory, including overnight and fringe shifts, running through the same months as coursework. What gives: the pace of the degree. Leeds permits breaks between modules; taking fewer modules during the service year is a use of the programme's design, not a lapse.

The art therapy placement against the doctoral application window

The VATI placement runs to the end of the eighteen months; SFU's application opens on 1 October and asks for a portfolio and a supervisor conversation. What gives: nothing, if the creative project is designed from the start to be the portfolio's first piece. If it was not, the application waits a year, which is a normal outcome rather than a signal.

Stage three's practicum against the salaried role

Daytime placements and a supervised clinical course one day a week cannot coexist with a senior full-time post. What gives: the role, in its current form — and the arrangement replacing it is made at the start of the stage, alongside the financial plan, rather than negotiated in the middle of a placement.

The floors that are never traded

One Make Space session weekly. One substantial piece of writing monthly. One kept studio Sunday weekly, and the catalogue entry for every sheet. These have a minimum that is defended and a maximum that is refused, in every stage including the worst one. They are not what the plan can spare — they are the raw material every later stage draws on.

Fixed dates, and what is not yet confirmed

What is set by others

Six things carry external dates. Everything else moves at whatever pace the work and the life allow. The verification items are ordered by what it would cost to be wrong about them, and each is checked with the source institution rather than a secondary summary.

Fixed date

31 December. Prerequisite courses must be complete by this date in the year of application to the counselling psychology master's. Every enrolment decision in Stage two anchors here.

Fixed date

March and September 2027. The two Leeds intakes. Missing one means the next; the programme allows up to four years to complete once started.

Fixed date

29 November 2027. Psychotherapy becomes a regulated profession in British Columbia under the College of Health and Care Professionals. Qualifying-programme standards are still being set and bear directly on the master's choice — and on whether RCC and RCAT are the registrations the plan ends on.

Fixed date

30 March, each year. The Vancouver Art Therapy Institute application deadline for a July start. Stage four is entered on the first 30 March after the counselling master's is conferred.

Fixed date

1 October. SFU's PhD in Contemporary Arts opens applications for the following September. Stage five is entered on the first October after the art therapy diploma is in hand and the portfolio has been shown.

Fixed date

Crisis Centre intake windows. Cohorts are limited and dates are fixed. This is the item most likely to set the shape of the next eighteen months, and the one to confirm first.

Verify first

The employer's tuition benefit and flexible-hours provisions at UBC and SFU, and specifically whether staff can enrol in the counselling master's while employed. This now decides which master's is applied to, and it is the cheapest available reduction to all three troughs. If neither holds, Adler and Athabasca are checked next.

Verify first

The Vancouver Art Therapy Institute's current prerequisites, distance-stream provisions, placement requirements, and fee; and the registration requirements for RCC with the BC Association of Clinical Counsellors and RCAT with the Canadian Art Therapy Association. All new to the plan.

Verify first

SFU's PhD in Interactive Arts and Technology — admission on a counselling master's plus a BFA and a design certificate, funding, part-time provisions, and which faculty work on accessibility and learning. New and unchecked.

Verify first

Whether UBC's Art History PhD admits on a counselling master's plus a BFA. Its stated norm is a relevant MA. If not, the doctoral stage is SFU only, and the MA thesis in Stage three matters more.

Verify first

Doctoral funding structures at SFU's School for the Contemporary Arts and UBC's Art History, Visual Art and Theory — package levels, SSHRC eligibility, assistantship rates, and whether either programme can be taken part-time alongside a university role. Stage five's entry condition cannot be assessed without these.

Verify first

Whether the counselling master's permits genuinely part-time enrolment, and how practicum hours are scheduled within it. The proposal to shallow the first trough by extending it depends entirely on this.

Verify first

Current tuition and practicum costs for each named programme, and the Leeds fee for 2027 entry — the published total applies to earlier starts and will not carry forward. Neither trough can be sized without these.

Verify first

Crisis Centre basic training cost, which volunteers pay themselves, and the comparable routes if declined — the Crisis Centre for Northern BC and Crisis Text Line both produce supervised distress work and a reference.

Verify first

Which prerequisites the earlier UBC coursework already satisfies, against the published equivalency table, and whether admissions testing is currently required. Departmental and calendar listings disagree, and the answer changes the preparation timeline by months.

How this document is kept

One review, twice a year, with two questions: is this stage finished, and has any cell in the matrix changed level. Between reviews the plan is not reopened — which is what makes it possible to put the whole of a day's attention into the day's actual work.
