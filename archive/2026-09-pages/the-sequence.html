The Sequence — Living Document

A path measured in readiness,
not in years.

One reconciled order of work, replacing the two that had drifted apart — revised in September 2026 so that phases three to six follow career.html. Each phase closes on a condition met rather than a date reached — so the plan holds whether it takes six years or fourteen, and so no part of it is carried at a pace that would make the work worse.

Why this is not a timeline. The earlier plans set each stage against a year, and years turned out to be the wrong unit. A part-time credential taken alongside a full working life, a facilitation practice, and a studio does not move at a predictable rate, and it should not be asked to. Keyed to years, a plan generates a permanent low-grade sense of being behind. Keyed to conditions, it generates a clear answer to one question: is this stage finished, and am I ready for the next.

Nothing here expires. Two items carry real external dates, and they are listed at the end so they do not have to be held in mind anywhere else.

Before the first phase

The path does not start from zero. It starts from here.

Any sequence that opens with a credential implies that the work begins when the credential does. That is not the case, and stating what is already in hand removes the largest distortion in the earlier documents — the sense of a long runway before anything counts.

Fifteen years of practice

Learning systems across higher education, research institutions, and the public sector. The rarest component of the profile and the one already complete.

Two completed foundations

The BFA as method rather than history; two research-based years of neurobiology as the biological substrate.

A running practice

Make Space in weekly delivery, the Other Bodies tools in use, and a year-long curriculum already tested with real participants.

A published body of work

Fifty-odd mapped contributions across five areas of expertise, with sources, and two public writing practices in continuous operation.

Phase one Ground

Put the existing work on the record, and get inside a university.

Nothing in this phase requires enrolment, permission, or a change in circumstances. It is the phase that makes every later application easier, and it is entirely composed of work already begun.

The work of this phase

  • A role inside health or accessibility. A health authority, research institute, or an accessibility post in higher education or the public sector. The sector matters more than the title.
  • Outcome measurement in Make Space. A validated depression screen at the start and end of each cycle. The smallest change with the largest downstream effect: it turns a practice into a program with a defensible claim.
  • The conceptual paper, submitted. No affiliation required. The argument that the training system delivering an intervention matters as much as the intervention.
  • Four accessibility resources published. The anchor on universal design and individual accommodation, essential requirements, episodic conditions, and access in clinical placement. All four are written; none are public. One caveat before they go live: the essential-requirements piece was drafted before the AFMC published its technical standards reform in May 2025, and will carry a claim of scarcity that is no longer accurate. An hour's revision, not a rewrite — but it should happen before publication rather than after.
  • CPACC. Self-paced, inexpensive, and the credential that makes the accessibility expertise legible to institutions that need a formal marker.

This phase is complete when

  • The conceptual paper is submitted or under review.
  • One Make Space cohort has pre and post data.
  • The four accessibility resources are live.
  • The working role is inside the sector, and stable enough to study alongside.

If this takes eighteen months, that is the correct length. If it takes three years because the right role took time to find, the plan is not late — the condition simply was not met yet.

Carried

The role, the writing, Make Space at its weekly rhythm, the studio practice at its quarterly rhythm.

Not yet

No coursework. This phase deliberately holds no enrolment, so that the publishing and the job search have room.

Phase two Frame

The theoretical grounding, and enough research literacy to read the field.

Leeds is online, part-time and modular, and permits pauses between modules — which is what makes it compatible with a working life. The five undergraduate prerequisites for the counselling master's run in the same phase, as evening and online courses, and where the two collide the prerequisites win — anything with a fixed external date outranks anything without one. The LSHTM courses that used to sit here were set aside in August 2026; statistics survives as one of the prerequisites, which is where the ability to read a depression trial actually comes from.

The work of this phase

  • MSc Disability Studies, Rights and Inclusion — Leeds, online. A two-week induction, then nine 15-credit taught modules of eight weeks each, with breaks permitted between them. The final piece is not a dissertation: it is a 45-credit applied project over 24 weeks, and it may either address a specific challenge in partnership with a disability rights organisation, service provider, or policy or practice organisation — an employer counts — or be a research project making recommendations for policy, programmes or professional practice. Both routes fit work already underway: the corpus study is the second, and piloting an essential-requirements review with a professional programme is the first.
  • A Global Mental Health certificate, scoped. Harvard's Trauma and Recovery programme or a King's or LSHTM short course, inside a module break, for one purpose: the task-shifting evidence the Make Space training system rests on. The master's stays set aside; the certificate is the part of that track that serves the plan.
  • The five undergraduate prerequisites. Interviewing skills, counselling theories, learning or cognitive psychology, abnormal psychology, and statistics — checked against the department's equivalency table before anything is enrolled in, and complete by 31 December in the year of application.
  • A qualitative paper from Make Space. Two or three years of documentation is enough for a thematic or phenomenological study of participant experience. Submittable without a doctoral affiliation.

This phase is complete when

  • The Leeds applied project is submitted.
  • A trial's methods and results sections can be read without deferring to someone else.
  • A second paper is submitted or published.
  • The registration question has been answered — see the forks below.

Leeds permits breaks between modules. Taking one is a use of the programme's design, not a lapse in the plan.

Carried

The role, coursework, one paper, the Substacks at minimum viable rhythm, Make Space, the studio.

Set down

New consulting scope. The practice holds existing clients; it does not grow during coursework.

The earlier documents disagreed about this degree. One listed it as optional and post-doctoral with five alternatives and an explicit option to skip. Another made it a required step occupying two years and pushing everything after it back. A third assumed throughout that it was happening.

This sequence resolves it as optional, and decided later. The three short courses deliver the literacy the plan actually asks for. The full master's delivers a thesis, a network, and formal standing in global health — all valuable, none required before the room. If the international thread becomes central during the doctoral phase, the network is available through fellowships and short-course clusters without another degree.

Phase three The room

Clinical formation, and the registration that opens the room.

This is the phase the earlier plans understated. A counselling master's is cohort-based, with several hundred supervised practicum hours in daytime placements. It cannot be carried alongside a senior full-time role unless the employer is the school — which is why the Ground phase now targets UBC and SFU by name. The fork this document once left open, registration or research authority, is closed here: registration as a clinical counsellor is the clinical destination, and research authority comes later, through the studio.

The work of this phase

  • A counselling master's — the MA or the MEd. UBC or SFU if a staff member can study there while employed; Adler University's Master of Counselling Psychology or Athabasca's online Master of Counselling if not. Disability and depression named as the focus from the application onward. Practicum placements requested in post-secondary counselling and disability services.
  • A worked financial plan, made before applying. Reduced hours, a secondment, contract work, or a leave. This is a planning task, not a compromise, and it belongs at the start of the phase rather than in the middle of it.
  • Registration as a clinical counsellor. Applied for on conferral. This is the registration the practice opens under.
  • The studio kept on its standing order. Sundays where the programme allows; the catalogue always. Two phases from here a doctoral committee reads it.

This phase is complete when

  • The degree is conferred and the practicum hours are complete.
  • The RCC application is submitted.
  • The salary has survived the phase in some form, or the reason it did not is written down.

This phase is no longer a possible destination, as the earlier version said it might be. The art therapy diploma requires it, and the doctorate that follows is in a different faculty altogether. What it is, plainly, is the registration the rest of the life practises under.

Carried

Clinical training first. Make Space at minimum rhythm, the studio on its Sundays, the Substacks reduced to monthly.

Set down

The full-time senior role, in its current form, unless the employer is the school. Consulting becomes the flexible income rather than the employment.

Phase four The second room

The BFA brought into clinical service, and the practice opened.

New to the sequence, and the shortest phase in it. The Advanced Diploma in Art Therapy at the Vancouver Art Therapy Institute: eighteen months from a July start, applications by 30 March, a 600-hour placement, a creative project at the end, and registration as a Registered Canadian Art Therapist after it. The Institute requires a completed counselling master's, which fixes this phase's place in the order. The earlier documents held this credential twenty years out, to be taken up if it still called. It is calling now.

The work of this phase

  • The diploma, distance stream. Blended coursework with short residential intensives in Vancouver, and a placement requested in post-secondary or community mental health with a disability population.
  • The creative project as the first piece of the doctoral portfolio. Designed that way from the start, so that this phase and the next share their work rather than compete for it.
  • Therapeutic Studio opened as a practice. At RCC scope first, deliberately small, with art therapy added on registration. The naming question — whether Make Space and a clinical practice can share the name — is settled before the first client.

This phase is complete when

  • The diploma is held and the RCAT registration is granted.
  • The first clients have been seen at Therapeutic Studio.
  • The body of studio work has been shown, seriously, at least once.

This is the phase in which the clinical life becomes real and paid. Everything after it is optional in the way the earlier version said the doctorate was — and, unlike the earlier version, the thing that is optional is the thing most wanted.

Carried

The diploma first. The practice small. Make Space at its floor. The studio at full, because the creative project is studio work.

Set down

Nothing new. The retainer carries the placement months; no new consulting scope is taken.

Phase five The question

A doctorate, on the studio's own terms.

The earlier plans specified a counselling psychology doctorate — the protected title, a CPA internship, and a dissertation on access and depression. That doctorate is no longer on the plan. What replaces it is a PhD in Contemporary Arts at SFU, which supports practice-based theses and was founded to give practising artists a doctoral credential where the MFA had been the ceiling, or a PhD in Art History at UBC. The question is Dear Ordinary's — the ordinary body, grief, and the sacred — held inside the scholarly frame the rest of the path built.

The work of this phase

  • A doctorate chosen for the lectern, not the title. SFU's programme runs twelve to fifteen semesters, matches a supervisor on admission, and examines a practice-based thesis with a major work shown publicly. UBC's is the scholarly route, and whether it admits on a counselling master's plus a BFA is unverified.
  • A supervisor identified before a programme is. Relationships first, applications second. The portfolio and the shown work are what the conversation is about.
  • Teaching assistantships from the first year. They are both the funding and the teaching record an adjunct appointment is later assessed on.
  • The practice held small, both registrations kept current. Enough hours to keep the room alive; not enough to compete with the thesis.

This phase is complete when

  • There is a body of work and a question that cannot be finished without doctoral resources.
  • A supervisor exists who wants the work.
  • The funding is confirmed, or the salary can survive the programme at a fraction — established before entry, not inside it.
  • Both registrations are held and the practice is running.

If those four are never all true at once, not entering is a legitimate outcome — not a failure of the plan. Two registrations, the accessibility role, and sessional studio teaching are most of the life without it. What the doctorate adds is the adjunct appointment and the faculty stream.

Carried

The thesis, which is the studio. A reduced caseload. The accessibility role at whatever fraction the programme permits.

Set down

Everything expandable. Consulting, facilitation growth, and new writing commitments hold at their floors.

Phase six Deployment

The room, the studio, the lectern, and the method, running together.

The fractional life one of my mentors holds: a primary university career, a clinical registration and a practice, an adjunct appointment in an art department, and public service. Nothing here requires a new post to be created. It requires four existing kinds of post to be held by one person.

The work of this phase

  • The accessibility role, senior. The salaried spine, and the seat from which essential requirements get rewritten.
  • Therapeutic Studio at RCC and RCAT scope. Depression, disability, and image. Sized by desire rather than need.
  • Sessional, then adjunct, at SFU's School for the Contemporary Arts or UBC's Art History, Visual Art and Theory. Studio and seminar teaching, and supervision of graduate students whose work sits where mine does.
  • Make Space manualised, trained out, and evaluated. The learning-systems method published and applied to the training curriculum.

This phase is complete when

  • It does not close. This is the practice.

The marker that proves the thesis is not a date. It is a single year in which all four seats are held at once, none of them full-time, and the studio is the thing the others are arranged around rather than the thing they crowd out.

Carried

All of it, at chosen volumes. The studio expands here; it is the only stream that does.

Set down

Anything that would make one seat full-time at the expense of the others. The test for a consulting enquiry: would I take this if I did not need the money?

Open questions

One fork closed, and two this document does not close for you.

The first was the largest open question in every earlier version of this document, and the September 2026 revision closed it. The other two have been left open deliberately. What follows is what each choice costs and what it buys, stated evenly, so the decision can be made once with the information visible rather than re-litigated at every review.

Is registration the destination, or is research authority?

Closed, September 2026. Both — but in different rooms. Registration as a clinical counsellor and art therapist is the clinical destination; research authority comes through an art doctorate rather than a counselling one. The cost of that answer is the protected psychologist title and the counselling faculty stream, and it is named as given up in career.html rather than left ambiguous here.

What it bought

A doctorate on a question that is mine, taken from a portfolio rather than a thesis; the studio as a first-class stream a decade sooner than the earlier plan allowed; and a lectern in an art department.

What it cost

The psychologist tier at the campus counselling centres, the CPA internship, and the educational leadership stream in a counselling department. A counsellor's scope is the clinical ceiling. If that ceiling turns out to matter, the counselling doctorate is not closed — it is simply no longer next.

Which counselling master's?

Open, and decided by one fact: whether a UBC or SFU staff member can take the programme while employed. The MA or the MEd no longer matters to the next credential, since the art therapy diploma and the art doctorate both accept either.

If the salary and the school can coexist

UBC or SFU, from inside the employer, with the tuition benefit and a practicum negotiated around a four-day week. The cheapest version of the phase there is.

If they cannot

Adler University's Master of Counselling Psychology in Vancouver, built for working students — and worth checking whether its art therapy specialisation would fold phase four into this one — or Athabasca's online Master of Counselling. Neither is second-best. Both produce the RCC.

Making, writing about making — or designing?

SFU's PhD in Contemporary Arts examines a practice-based thesis; UBC's PhD in Art History examines a scholarly one; SFU's PhD in Interactive Arts and Technology examines design research, and is the one door where accessibility is the thesis rather than the frame. The choice is not about prestige. It is about which room I want to be examined in and teach from — and it is made at the close of phase four, by whichever record is stronger by then, the studio's or the design portfolio's.

SFU · practice-based

The studio is the thesis. The culminating work is shown. The cohort is artists. Entry reads the portfolio and the shown work, which is why the studio-day rhythm starts now and not later.

SFU SIAT · design research

Admits on academic preparation, fit with a research faculty member, and a supervisory agreement made before applying; roughly one applicant in ten is offered a place, and the school is on the Surrey campus. The strongest transfer case on the ladder — accessible learning systems go straight to institutions — and the furthest from the studio as a practice. The design certificate in phase one is what makes this door real.

UBC · scholarly

The writing is the thesis. Entry may require a master's in art history rather than in counselling, which is unverified and would decide this fork on its own. If admitted, it is the route to the seminar room rather than the studio.

External clocks

The only four things here that carry a date.

Everything else in this document moves at whatever pace the work and the life allow. These four are set by other people, so they are recorded once, here, and do not need to be held anywhere else.

VATI application — 30 March

The Vancouver Art Therapy Institute takes applications by 30 March for a July start. Phase four is entered on the first 30 March after the counselling master's is conferred.

SFU PhD applications — 1 October

The School for the Contemporary Arts opens doctoral applications on 1 October for the following September. Phase five is entered on the first October after the diploma is in hand and the portfolio has been shown.

Leeds intake

The online MSc runs to fixed application windows with more than one start point in the year. Worth confirming the current cycle directly with Leeds before assuming a date — and worth confirming which school administers it, since listings currently disagree between the School of Sociology and Social Policy and the School of Education. Missing a window simply means the next one.

Psychotherapy regulation in British Columbia

The Health Professions and Occupations Act came into force on 1 April 2026. Psychotherapy becomes a regulated profession on 29 November 2027, under the College of Health and Care Professionals of BC.

The detail that matters more than the date: what is being regulated is the title. From that date, only registrants may call themselves a psychotherapist. Practitioners who do not use the protected title are expected to be able to continue practising. Registration is therefore a decision about standing and scope rather than a precondition for doing the work — which is relevant to the first fork below. Qualifying-programme standards are still being set, so confirm them directly before choosing a master's, and check the college's own publications rather than secondary summaries, which currently disagree about the eventual name of the regulating body.

A plan keyed to years turns every ordinary month into evidence of falling behind. A plan keyed to conditions asks a smaller and answerable question: is this stage finished. The work is already underway, it has been for fifteen years, and the pace at which the remaining pieces arrive changes very little about what it eventually amounts to.
