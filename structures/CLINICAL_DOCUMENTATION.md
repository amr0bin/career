# 🗂️ Clinical Documentation — Intake, Plan, Progress Notes, Closing Summary

> **The everyday records of counselling practice, written to be accurate, minimal, useful and defensible.**
> Clinical records exist for the client's care, for continuity if someone else picks up the work, and for accountability. They are read by supervisors, by colleges in a complaint, and by clients themselves, who may request their file. Write every note as if the client will read it.

---

## 1. The structure

| Part | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Intake / assessment summary | Presenting concern, relevant history, risk screen, strengths, consent, initial formulation | Informed consent and limits of confidentiality documented first |
| Treatment or service plan | Goals agreed with the client, approach, frequency, review date | Goals in the client's words |
| Progress notes | One per session: what was worked on, observations, interventions, response, plan | DAP or SOAP format; brief, factual, dated, signed |
| Risk documentation | Any risk assessment, the reasoning and the actions taken | Record consultation with supervisor |
| Correspondence and consultation log | Contacts with others, with consent recorded |  |
| Closing summary | Reason for ending, progress against goals, referrals, follow-up | Written within a set period of the last session |

---

## 2. How to do it well

1. Pick one note format (DAP: Data, Assessment, Plan) and use it every time.
2. Write the note the same day, in 10 minutes or less.
3. Describe behaviour and words, not judgements: *'said she has not slept more than four hours since Monday'*, not *'appeared manic'*.
4. Record only what serves care and accountability. Process material and your own reactions go to supervision, not to the file.
5. Know the retention rules and the client's right of access before the first client.
6. For art therapy: record artwork by description and photograph per consent policy; the artwork belongs to the client.

- Build templates for the six documents once, in the practicum, and carry them into Therapeutic Studio.
- Practise only on invented clients until the practicum (house rule 2: nothing client-derived, ever, in any public piece).
