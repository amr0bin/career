# 📈 Program Evaluation & Practice-Reflection Genres

> **How to show what a programme like Make Space does, honestly, without passing practice off as research.**
> Community practice can be written up in three legitimate genres: as a programme evaluation, as practice reflection or autoethnography, or as research under an ethics board. The choice changes the design, the consent language and the venue, and must be made before data are collected.

---

## 1. The structure

| Part | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose and users | Who will use the evaluation, for what decision | Utilisation-focused |
| Logic model | Inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes | One page |
| Evaluation questions | Three to five, tied to decisions |  |
| Methods | Attendance, session notes, feedback forms; validated measures only under an ethics route | House rule 1 |
| Findings | Against each question |  |
| Use | What will change because of the evaluation |  |

---

## 2. How to do it well

1. Decide the genre first: evaluation (quality improvement, usually outside REB review in TCPS 2 Article 2.5), reflection or autoethnography (your own experience), or research (needs an REB).
2. Build a logic model before collecting anything.
3. Collect only what the questions need; nothing client-derived in public pieces, ever.
4. If the work later becomes research, start new consent under the new route; do not reuse evaluation data as research data.

## 🧭 Credible examples, fast path and where this sits now

### 📚 Credible examples

- CDC. *Framework for Program Evaluation in Public Health* (updated 2024).
- Patton, M. Q. (2008). *Utilization-Focused Evaluation* (4th ed.). Sage.
- Glasgow, R. E., Vogt, T. M., & Boles, S. M. (1999). Evaluating the public health impact of health promotion interventions: the RE-AIM framework. *American Journal of Public Health, 89*(9), 1322–1327.
- Ellis, C., Adams, T. E., & Bochner, A. P. (2011). Autoethnography: an overview. *Forum Qualitative Sozialforschung / Forum: Qualitative Social Research, 12*(1), Art. 10.
- TCPS 2 (2022), Article 2.5: quality assurance and programme evaluation.

### ⚡ Do it well and fast

- A one-page logic model and three questions are enough to start.
- The weekly Make Space session note *is* the evaluation's raw material.
