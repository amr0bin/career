# 🔁 Knowledge Translation (KT) Plan

> **A plan for getting findings to the people who set the process, written into the study from the start.**
> Funders (CIHR especially) score the KT plan. More importantly, your thesis question only matters if an accessibility office changes a process. The plan says who needs to know, what, in what form, and how you will know it landed.

---

## 1. The structure

| Part | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Integrated KT (knowledge users as partners throughout) or end-of-grant KT | Your design is integrated: DPO partner, co-researcher |
| Knowledge users | Named audiences: students, advisors, directors, policy owners |  |
| Key messages | Two or three, per audience |  |
| Activities and products | Brief, workshop, plain-language summary, paper, tool | See `POLICY_BRIEF.md` and `PUBLIC_SCHOLARSHIP.md` |
| Timeline and resources | When, who, cost |  |
| Evaluation | How uptake will be known | Adoption by other institutions is the evidence |

---

## 2. How to do it well

1. Name knowledge users by role and, where possible, by office.
2. Involve them from the question stage (integrated KT); the advisory group already does this.
3. Write a plain-language summary at a Grade 8 reading level for every output.
4. Make every product accessible in format, not only content.
