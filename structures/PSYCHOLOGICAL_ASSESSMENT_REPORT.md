# 📐 Psychological Assessment Report

> **The integrated written report that answers a referral question from tests, interview and history.**
> Assessment is a core competency of a counselling psychologist, and the assessment report is examined throughout the PhD, the internship and registration. Its discipline is integration: answering the referral question, not reporting test scores one by one.

---

## 1. The structure

| Part | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Identifying information and referral question | Who, referred by whom, and exactly what they want to know | The question governs everything that follows |
| Procedures | Interviews, collateral sources, every instrument with its date |  |
| Background | Relevant history only | Developmental, educational, medical, social, cultural |
| Behavioural observations | How the person engaged with testing; validity of results |  |
| Results and interpretation | Integrated by *domain*, not by test | Converging and diverging evidence |
| Summary and formulation | Answer to the referral question | Strengths as well as needs; diagnosis only if warranted and within scope |
| Recommendations | Specific, practical, tied to findings | Including environmental and access recommendations |

---

## 2. How to do it well

1. Rewrite a vague referral question into an answerable one before testing.
2. Choose instruments for the question, with norms appropriate to the person (culture, language, disability).
3. Interpret by domain, triangulating at least two sources for every conclusion.
4. State the limits: accommodations used during testing and how they affect norms.
5. Write recommendations a teacher, a client or an advisor can implement.
6. Read the report aloud as the client: is anything stigmatising or unnecessary?

## 🧭 Credible examples, fast path and where this sits now

### 📚 Credible examples

- Lichtenberger, E. O., Mather, N., Kaufman, N. L., & Kaufman, A. S. (2004). *Essentials of Assessment Report Writing*. Wiley.
- Groth-Marnat, G., & Wright, A. J. (2016). *Handbook of Psychological Assessment* (6th ed.). Wiley.
- AERA, APA, & NCME. (2014). *Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing*.

### ⚡ Do it well and fast

- Use a domain template (cognitive, academic, emotional, functional, environmental).
- Write the summary first, then build the domain sections that support it.
