# 🪪 Professional Registration & Licensing Exams

> **The applications and exams that convert degrees into the right to practise.**
> Each stage of the arc ends in a registration: RCC after the MA, RCAT after VATI, psychologist after the PhD. Each has a written application, documented hours and, for psychology, national and jurisdictional exams. Plan to the higher bar from the start.

---

## 1. The structure

| Part | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility audit | Coursework areas, practicum hours, supervision hours against the body's list | Do it in MA Year 1 |
| Documentation | Transcripts, course outlines, supervised-hours forms, supervisor attestations | Keep outlines for every course |
| Application | Forms, references, statements, fees | Submit the week of conferral |
| Exams | EPPP (psychology); jurisprudence; any oral exam | Retrieval practice, timed sets |
| Maintenance | Continuing education, insurance, renewal |  |

---

## 2. How to do it well

1. Map every course against the body's required areas in MA Year 1 (RCC needs six core areas, including group and family therapy).
2. Log supervision as it happens, with signatures, in the Private Supervision Log.
3. Track regulatory change: psychotherapy becomes regulated in BC on 29 Nov 2027 under the CHCPBC, and designations may not carry over automatically.
4. For psychology, sit the EPPP and jurisprudence exams during the internship year so registration runs alongside the postdoc.

## 🧭 Credible examples, fast path and where this sits now

### 📚 Credible examples

- BC Association of Clinical Counsellors (BCACC): RCC requirements.
- Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association (CCPA): CCC designation.
- College of Health and Care Professionals of BC (CHCPBC): psychology applicant requirements and psychotherapy regulation.
- Association of State and Provincial Psychology Boards (ASPPB): *EPPP* candidate handbook.
- Canadian Art Therapy Association: RCAT requirements.

### ⚡ Do it well and fast

- Keep one spreadsheet of requirements per body, updated each spring.
- Use the question bank from `WRITTEN_EXAMINATION.md` for the EPPP.
