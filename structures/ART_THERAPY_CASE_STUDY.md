# 🎨 Art Therapy Case Study & Presentation

> **A case written from art therapy practice, holding image, process and theory together.**
> Art therapy training assesses the ability to show how image-making functioned in the therapeutic relationship. The case study is where studio knowledge, clinical reasoning and ethics meet.

---

## 2. How to do it well

1. Get explicit written consent for every image you might show, at the time, with the right to withdraw.
2. Describe the artwork phenomenologically first (what is there), then the process, then meaning.
3. Avoid fixed symbolic readings; treat meaning as made with the client.
4. Hold the social model in view: the image as a response to circumstances, not only to inner states.
