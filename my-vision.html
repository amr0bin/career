My Vision — Living Document · Revised 25 September 2026

Working on the thing that
matters most.

Disabled students report depression at higher rates than their non-disabled peers, and then meet a support structure that treats access and mental health as two separate offices with two separate intake forms. Very few people sit in either room able to see that a flattening mood and an unmet access need are the same problem viewed from two angles. This is the plan for becoming one of them, and then for building what that vantage point makes possible — one specialism, a clinical registration, a doctorate, and six stages, built from creativity, learning science, disability studies, neurobiology, and a decade of making knowledge travel.

The education path, as of 13 September 2026: one specialism already held, a thesis-based counselling master's and registration as a clinical counsellor, and a doctorate — counselling psychology, or a practice-based or art-history doctorate — chosen in the master's second year and entered funded. The art therapy diploma stays available as a second registration. Every item below serves that path. The stages close when a condition is met rather than when a year elapses, and each one is described by what it opens before what it costs.

Each property has a defined minimum viable state for the busiest stretches — for the personal site, a current CV and publication list; for the practice, a static offer page with the deposited research artefacts; for Therapeutic Studio, the notebook; for the studio, one kept Sunday and the catalogue.

The gap this closes is not a gap in compassion or in funding. It is a gap in combined expertise, and it is unusually narrow — which is exactly why filling it is worth a career.

I am building toward becoming a registered clinician and doctoral researcher whose specialism is disability — able to hold a clinical hour with a student whose mood is flattening, to read that student's programme's essential requirements and say precisely why they are indefensible, and to produce the evidence that changes them. The counselling centre addresses the distress. The accessibility office addresses the accommodation. I intend to build the place where they are seen as one problem.

Unusual competitive advantage — the intersections that matter

The fields that sit between these pairs are where underserved, high-value work exists. Most researchers and practitioners live in one. This path builds fluency across all of them — in service of one problem.

Disability + depression Clinical hour + policy document Accommodation law + clinical practice Essential requirements + construct validity Learning design + counselling Neuroscience + expressive arts Research + direct practice Art + clinical science Universal design + mental health access Evidence + embodied knowledge

The reach is wider than a caseload. A student helped in the counselling room is one person. A professional programme's technical standards rewritten from method to construct changes who can enter that profession at all, for every cohort that follows. A centre that joins the two offices changes what every disabled student meets on arrival. The same expertise does all three. The doctorate is the instrument by which one person's practice becomes evidence that other institutions adopt without me in the room.

Challenge 01Timeline

Pace across many streams

Challenge 02Evidence

What it opens: an unsettled evidence base is where a research career makes its largest contribution. Someone with clinical registration, disability expertise, a neurobiology foundation, and a running programme with outcome data is positioned to produce the mechanism and access evidence the field is missing.
