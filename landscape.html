A learning design practice, mapped

Much of what people need in order to act — on their health, their rights, their work — is not missing but unusable: built without them, for conditions that are not theirs. Learning design is where those choices are made. This practice develops knowledge through interaction with the people it concerns, and treats access as a condition of the work rather than a correction to it. The map sets out part of that practice: a trunk of shared craft and five branches of expertise, with lines where they draw on the same theory and method.

A text version of this map presents the same content in reading order, without the diagram, for screen reader and keyboard use.
