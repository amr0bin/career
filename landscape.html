Learning Design SpecializationInteractive map

A learning design practice, mapped

Five areas of expertise — learning experience design, universal design for learning, knowledge mobilization, learning systems design, and accessibility, disability and accommodation. Each circle is a piece of applied work developed with learners, design teams, and partner organizations. The lines between them show where the areas draw on shared theory and shared method.

A text version of this map presents the same content in reading order, without the diagram, for screen reader and keyboard use.

How to read this. Two arrangements answer two questions. By area shows where each contribution lives. By affinity repositions the same nodes by how alike they are, so relationships across areas surface — the evaluation family, the equity thread, the community line. Select any circle to read what it contributes, its sources, and the contributions most like it. A few contributions belong to two areas at once; each is shown once, in its home colour, and says so when selected — so an area page may list more resources than the map colours for it.
