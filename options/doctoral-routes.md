## The decision, stated once

**Decided 31 October 2026. The doctorate is the UBC PhD in Counselling Psychology** (target entry ~2035, funded, with a supervisor who wants the question). The art doctorate is considered and set aside. It is not planned, and it is not a hedge.

The earlier note (`05-the-two-doctoral-routes.md`, 13 September 2026) held both routes open until the MA's second year. Most of its reasoning still stands: a doctorate is wanted, the work is entered funded or not at all, and the studio continues whichever doctorate is chosen. What changed is that the facts it was waiting for have now been verified, and they all point one way.

## Why counselling psychology

**1. It is the only BC route to the psychologist title.** Registration as a psychologist in BC requires a doctorate. UBC's PhD in Counselling Psychology is CPA-accredited and the only counselling psychology doctorate in the province. SFU has none; its PhD in Educational Psychology does not lead to registration. (**verified** 6 Oct 2026, chcpbc.org/applicants/psychology; grad.ubc.ca; sfu.ca/education)

**2. The title brings diagnostic authority, and that authority is the lever.** A registered psychologist in BC may diagnose, and UBC accepts learning-disability documentation only from a registered psychologist or a certified school psychologist. Someone who holds that authority and also knows accommodation law, essential requirements and disability studies stands on both sides of the gate that decides who gets access. That is the ceiling: redesigning the documentation gate itself. (**verified**, B.C. Reg. 131/2025; students.ubc.ca)

**3. Service leadership.** UBC Counselling Services' leadership tier holds a PhD, EdD or R.Psych almost without exception. *The master's gets the room. The doctorate gets the service.* The joined access-and-mental-health centre at the top of the plan is a service-leadership seat.

**4. The research-chair path.** A funded research programme with doctoral students, and in time a chair on disability, accommodation and depression, needs a doctorate in the discipline where the question lives. The thesis question (*does the accommodation process itself drive depression in disabled students?*) is a counselling psychology question with a measurement core. UBC is where depression and measurement live (see `people/faculty-map.md`).

**5. Funding can be named.** UBC's minimum doctoral funding is $40,000 a year for four years; CGRS D, Killam, the Four Year Fellowship and Trudeau sit above it. Figures are in `facts/stability-and-figures.md`.

**What it requires, early:** a **thesis-based** MA with an 80%+ average, a supervisor relationship, and a publication record. The thesis-MA rule is why the MA entry is the thesis route (`options/programmes-compared.md`).

## The route set aside: an art doctorate

**What it was.** A practice-based PhD in Contemporary Arts at SFU (practice-based thesis with a major work shown publicly; 12 to 15 semesters; applications open 1 October), a design-research PhD at SFU's School of Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT; the one door where accessibility could be the thesis rather than the frame), or a scholarly PhD in Art History at UBC (AHVA).

**What it offered.**
- A doctorate on a question that is hers, admitted on a portfolio rather than a thesis.
- The studio as a first-class stream a decade sooner.
- A lectern in an art department: studio and seminar teaching, supervision of graduate students whose work sits where hers does.
- A natural continuation of the VATI art therapy diploma's creative project.

**Why set aside.** It gives up the psychologist title, diagnostic authority, the CPA internship and the counselling-service leadership tier: the four things the ceiling depends on. Its own admission question (whether any of the three doors admits on a BFA plus a counselling master's, without an MFA or art-history MA) was never verified.

**What is kept from it.** The studio continues on its standing order unchanged. A decision about a doctorate is exactly the kind of event that gets used as permission to let the studio lapse, and it is not one. The adjunct appointment in an art department stays in Stage 3's Optionality list, reached from the studio record and the VATI work rather than a doctorate. The VATI diploma stays on the plan (Stage 2, after the MA).

## What would reopen it

Any one of these, reviewed at the Stage 2 close (~2034):
- No UBC counselling supervisor wants the question after two funded application cycles, **and** an art-school supervisor does.
- The destination wanted has clearly become the art department's lectern rather than the service or the research chair.
- The studio question has outgrown what can be pursued outside a programme, and the catalogue and showings show it.
- A verified answer that SCA, SIAT or AHVA admits on a BFA plus a counselling master's, with funding.

Reopening means rereading this file and `05-the-two-doctoral-routes.md` against those four lines, not a mood.

## Fallback, named once

If the doctorate is not taken, RCC registration plus a counsellor or advisor role inside a university is a complete, secure career, with practice, sessional teaching and adjunct standing. The PhD widens the ceiling; it does not hold up the floor.
