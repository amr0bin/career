## 1 · UBC vs SFU counselling MA (apply both, 1 Dec 2030, for Sept 2031)

Both are applied to in the same cycle. UBC is the first choice because the doctorate is there; SFU is a serious parallel, not a backup.

| | **UBC** (ECPS, Faculty of Education) | **SFU** (Faculty of Education) |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis MA | 48 credits + 6-credit thesis (CNPS 599); a separate admission stream | 52 units incl. 18-unit thesis (EDUC 898); MA "with faculty approval" after entry |
| GRE | Required for the MA on central pages (**verify** at department) | Not required |
| Helping experience | 3 years (Calendar) / 2 years or 450 h (department page) — **verify** | ~2 years or 400 h |
| Prerequisites | 5 named (see `facts/requirements-by-gate.md`) | 6, all complete before applying |
| Shortlist | Video assessment **unverified** | Interview confirmed |
| Delivery | Full- or part-time permitted | Evenings, Burnaby and Surrey, built for working professionals |
| Practicum | CNPS 588 in department centres, then external placement | Surrey clinic, then Year 3 practicum, self-secured in Year 2 |
| CACEP | Accredited (fast-track to CCC; renewal past 30 Sep 2026 **verify**) | Not accredited (CCC by manual route) |
| Doctoral door | PhD Counselling Psychology, needs thesis MA at 80% | No counselling psychology doctorate |

Sources: grad.ubc.ca MA page; UBC Academic Calendar; sfu.ca application-tuition page (all **verified** 20 Sep 2026).

### The four differences that decide it

1. **The doctoral door is at UBC only.** The UBC PhD admits from "a thesis-based master's equivalent to the CNPS M.A." With the 31 Oct 2026 doctoral decision, this outranks every other row.
2. **SFU's thesis is a gate after entry, not a guarantee at admission.** "An MEd or an MA (with faculty approval)." If SFU is the offer taken, the supervisor conversation has to happen before enrolment and the MA approval is a Year 1 task. An SFU thesis MA would also need confirming as "equivalent to the CNPS M.A." for the UBC PhD.
3. **The GRE sits on UBC's thesis route.** The one route already committed to is the one carrying an admissions test. One email to ECPS answers it; until then, budget for it.
4. **CACEP changes how CCC is obtained, not whether.** UBC graduates are fast-tracked; SFU graduates submit course descriptions.

### Where SFU is stronger

- **The faculty cluster** for this question's method: community-engaged evaluation (Jordan), participatory prevention (Cameron), lived-experience partnership (Hou), and Birmingham's Autism Mentorship Initiative, which is disability as campus student experience.
- **The shape of the years**: evening classes beside a full-time role. Both programmes hit the same wall in the practicum year.

**What decides it in 2031:** an offer from a UBC supervisor who wants the question settles it. An SFU offer with a confirmed MA supervisor and written confirmation of PhD equivalence is a strong second.

## 2 · Thesis MA vs MEd

**Thesis MA, decided.** The UBC PhD requires it; the MEd at either school is coursework (UBC) or coursework plus a comprehensive exam (SFU). The MEd is excellent preparation for the room and closes the doctorate.

- The MEd's one advantage is that it skips the GRE at UBC.
- The UBC MEd plus a first-authored paper has been described as a thesis-equivalent route to the PhD (**verify**; it appears in the backups, not on a verified page).
- *What must not happen:* an MEd entered by default because it was easier to enter, or an SFU MA assumed rather than secured.

## 3 · Leeds MSc Disability Studies, Rights and Inclusion

Online, part-time, 24 months, from **Sept 2027** (intakes March and September 2027); breaks between modules permitted, four years maximum. £12,750 in total, the same for international students, payable by module. (**verified** 19 Sep / 6 Oct 2026, courses.leeds.ac.uk/d326)

**Structure:** a two-week induction; **nine fixed 15-credit modules** of eight weeks each (about 20 hours a week: 10 guided, 10 independent, plus a weekly webinar on UK time); then a **45-credit Applied Project**. There is no separate dissertation: the Applied Project is the final piece.

| Module | Type |
|---|---|
| Disability and Inequality | Foundation |
| Understanding Disability | Foundation |
| Disability and Inclusion | Foundation |
| Building Enabling Futures | Development |
| A Human Rights Approach to Disability | Development, core |
| Realising Disability Equality Through Policy | Development |
| Disability and Global Challenges | Development |
| Doing Disability Research | Development, core |
| Becoming Disability Change Makers | Development |
| **Applied Project (OSSP5300M)** | 45 credits, year two |

**The Applied Project** is done in partnership with an organisation (a disabled people's organisation or the employer), with Leeds as ethics host and supervisor. It is the bridge in the arc: Leeds project → MA thesis → PhD. Exit awards: PGCert (D328) and PGDip (D327) (**verify** for 2027).

**The rule:** every assessment takes one angle on the question and becomes a public piece within a month.

## 4 · Art therapy: VATI Advanced Diploma vs the alternatives

**Chosen: the VATI Advanced Diploma in Art Therapy, after the MA and before the PhD (~2034–35), alongside early RCC practice.** VATI requires a completed master's in counselling or a related field, which is why it cannot come earlier. Distance stream with residential intensives in Vancouver, a 600-hour placement, a creative project, and RCAT registration with the Canadian Art Therapy Association on completion. Applications by **30 March** for a July start.

Why VATI: it is post-master's, so it builds on the clinical training rather than repeating it; the distance format fits beside a counsellor role and practice; and it adds the second registration that makes Creative Health a clinical as well as a peer offer.

| Alternative | What it is | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|
| **Adler University** (Vancouver) | Master of Counselling Psychology, with an art therapy specialisation (**verify** it is current) | It replaces the MA rather than following it. Course-based, so it closes the UBC PhD |
| **Kutenai Art Therapy Institute** (Nelson, BC) | A graduate-level art therapy training programme, a long-established BC route to RCAT (**verify** current format, distance options, and whether it now requires a prior master's) | Location and format to verify. Kept as the alternative if VATI's intake or format does not fit 2034 |

**Contradiction, flagged:** the 31 Oct 2026 decision describes VATI as **~15 months**; career.html and the-sequence (Sept 2026) say **18 months from a July start**. Both are on the verify list; plan to 18.
