# Bottlenecks and backups

> **Purpose:** every known narrow point on the path, written as bottleneck → task → exit, in the order they are met. · **Governs:** what to do when a gate does not open on the first try. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Gates](https://app.notion.com/p/d4969021f8da4529a41eafec0c323ca4)

---

## The ceiling these protect

A counsellor and researcher inside UBC or SFU who holds the access lens and the mood lens at once, on the way to the PhD, the psychologist title and a joined access-and-mental-health service. Each bottleneck below is a task with a next step, not a reason to reconsider. The specialism, the record, the products and the registrations all travel across employers and provinces, so no single closed door costs the mission.

**Fallback, named once:** if every doctoral door stays closed, MA + RCC + a campus role already yields practice, sessional teaching and adjunct standing.

## What must not happen

From therapeutic-practice (Part eleven), each written as the task that prevents it:

| Must not happen | Task that prevents it |
|---|---|
| The January 2027 Crisis Centre cohort missed: it is the helping-experience clock and the first supervisor | Watch for the opening; three professional references ready before it opens |
| A participant, consent sheet, pre/post measure or instrument development before an ethics board and a supervisor exist | Make Space screening waits for an ethics route (see `options/open-forks.md`). Absolute |
| Identifiable participant material on any public surface | Nothing client- or participant-derived in any public repo or page, at any stage |
| The peer register and a clinical offer on the same page | Creative Health peer programme and Therapeutic Studio clinical practice kept visibly separate |
| The hours log lapsing | Logged within 24 hours of any service. Everything else can be done late |
| An MEd entered by default, or an SFU MA assumed rather than secured | Thesis route only; SFU MA approval as a Year 1 task |
| Group therapy and family therapy missed in electives | Both on the MA transcript, written into the plan now |
| The practicum year discovered rather than planned, financially | Practicum income question in the November review from 2030 |
| The prevalence claim published without a citation | Source it before any public statement of the question |
| AI used on the application itself | Statements, answers and video are hers alone; the system prepares the record and calendar |
