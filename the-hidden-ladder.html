The hidden ladder: what a strong candidate is eligible for.

Every stage of this path has doors that are not on the programme page. Most of them are opened by nomination, by a file that was started years earlier, or by knowing the door exists. This document lists them stage by stage, so that preparation begins before the opportunity is visible.

Written as a mentor who has walked a near-identical shape would write it — a counselling master's and clinical registration, an interdisciplinary doctorate, an adjunct appointment in an art department, and a primary university career held alongside. It is candid about which items are confirmed patterns and which are to be verified at source. Nothing here is a promise; all of it is preparable.

How this works

Three rules that govern every item below.

Most of these are won on a file, not in a room. Fellowships, committee seats, teaching appointments and community grants are decided by people reading a written record — a CV, a publication list, a shown catalogue, a teaching dossier, a nomination letter. A candidate who leads with Intellection, Learner and Input, and sits low on Woo and Command, should take that as good news: the file is the medium this profile is strongest in, and the plan already produces most of it as a by-product. What it does not produce on its own is the nomination.

Nomination is a system, and it has to be built. The largest awards on this ladder — Vanier, the university's flagship doctoral fellowships, adjunct appointments, public committee seats — are not applied for; they are nominated for by a department, a dean, or a sponsor. Someone has to think of you. For a profile that does not naturally work a room, that means keeping a short list of people who could nominate, telling each of them plainly what you are working toward, and sending them the shown record twice a year. Grace Wong Sneddon's career is as much a record of being nominated as of applying.

Every item is checked against the 80,000 Hours question. Does this add more good than the same hours spent on the practice, the writing, or the studio? Most items below pass because they are leverage rather than labour: a standards committee seat changes an entry requirement for every cohort; a funded doctoral year produces the work the lectern is assessed on; a community-engaged fellowship pays for research that would otherwise be done on evenings. The ones that do not pass are marked, and they are the ones to decline.

Confirmed pattern

The mechanism exists and the eligibility is known in general terms. Details — amounts, dates, current names — are still checked at source before anything is counted on.

Verify

Named from knowledge that may be dated, or inferred from how comparable institutions behave. The item is real enough to prepare for and not yet real enough to plan on.

Nomination

Cannot be applied for directly. The preparation is the file and the nominator, and it starts a stage early.

Decline

Looks like an opportunity, costs more good than it produces for this profile at this stage. Named so it does not have to be re-decided.

Stage one · Ground

Doors that are open with no credential at all.

The items in this stage are the ones most often missed, because nothing on the programme page says they exist. Every one of them is available to a practitioner with fifteen years of applied work and no graduate degree, and several of them are the seats the whole path is aimed at, reachable now in miniature.

Confirmed pattern

Accessibility standards committees. Accessibility Standards Canada convenes technical committees under the Accessible Canada Act, including on education and employment; the CSA Group runs accessibility standards committees; British Columbia has a Provincial Accessibility Committee under its Accessibility Act. Members are practitioners and people with lived experience, appointed on a written application. This is the essential-requirements work at the scale of a country, and it needs no degree. Apply for a technical committee or a standards working group in Stage one, and keep the seat.

Confirmed pattern

Sessional and continuing-studies teaching at an art or design school, on practice alone. Emily Carr, Langara, and the continuing-studies arms of UBC and SFU appoint studio instructors on a BFA and a practice, not a PhD. A single course — accessible drawing, the ordinary body, foundations — starts the teaching dossier six years before the doctorate needs one, and it is the first run of the Foundations module the studio-day site holds for 2028.

Confirmed pattern

The disability arts network. Kickstart Disability Arts & Culture in Vancouver, Tangled Art + Disability in Toronto, and the Canada Council's Deaf and Disability Arts funding stream — including Access Support, which pays disability-related costs on top of any grant — are where Dear Ordinary belongs and where it is least crowded. A first showing inside this network is the beginning of the shown record the doctorate is admitted on.

Verify

BC Arts Council and Canada Council individual artist grants. Project and professional-development grants for practising artists; eligibility rests on a professional practice record, which the catalogue and the quarterly showings are building. Check the current programme names, cycles, and whether a BFA plus fifteen years of exhibition history qualifies as professional status under their definitions.

Confirmed pattern

Practitioner journals that accept work without affiliation. The Journal of Postsecondary Education and Disability, the Canadian Journal of Disability Studies, Disability Studies Quarterly, the Canadian Journal of Art Therapy, and Arts in Psychotherapy all publish practitioner and conceptual work. The essential-requirements corpus study belongs in the first; the Make Space qualitative paper in the fourth or fifth. A publication before enrolment changes the tenor of every application after it.

Confirmed pattern

The post-secondary access community. CACUSS's accessibility and inclusion communities, NEADS, and the IAAP once the CPACC is held. These are where accessibility advisor posts are advertised before they are advertised, and where the people who will later sit on hiring committees are met in writing first.

Nomination

A nominators file. Not an opportunity — the mechanism for every nominated item on this ladder. Five people, named, who know what you are working toward and receive the shown record twice a year: a current mentor, a former employer, a Leeds tutor once enrolled, a clinical supervisor once placed, a studio faculty member once met. Started now, because the first nomination is asked for in Stage three.

Decline

Conference circuits for their own sake. One presentation a year, at the venue nearest the next application, is enough. A profile low on Woo does not convert conference attendance into opportunity efficiently; it converts written work into opportunity. Spend the travel budget on the paper.

Stage two · Leeds

A British university opens a second country's doors.

Enrolment at Leeds makes you a graduate student in the UK's founding disability studies centre. That status has uses beyond the degree, most of them unmentioned by the programme, and several of them are worth more than the modules.

Confirmed pattern

The Centre for Disability Studies' own seminar and conference circuit. Leeds runs and hosts disability studies events, and the Nordic Network on Disability Research and the Society for Disability Studies both have student rates and student presentation streams. Present the corpus study once, online, as a Leeds student. It is the cheapest international standing available on this path.

Verify

Leeds student prizes and dissertation awards. Most UK schools award a prize for the best applied project in a programme, and a Leeds prize on a disability-studies file is a line the counselling master's and the doctorate both read. Check whether the School of Sociology and Social Policy runs one, and what it is judged on, in the first module.

Confirmed pattern

Employer professional-development funds. Staff at UBC and SFU have access to union and association professional-development funds, and in some cases tuition waivers or fee reductions for study at their own institution. Leeds fees will not be covered; the prerequisites and the CPACC may be. This is checked before the Stage one offer is accepted, not after.

Confirmed pattern

The applied project as a funded partnership. The Leeds project may be done in partnership with an organisation, and an employer counts. A university accessibility office that commissions its own essential-requirements review, done inside a supervised programme, gets a credited study and the graduate student gets institutional standing for the work. Propose it to the employer in the first year.

Verify

Mitacs, early. Mitacs Accelerate funds graduate research internships with a partner organisation — a disability organisation, a health authority, a professional regulator. It normally requires enrolment at a Canadian institution, which Leeds is not; but the counselling master's is. Know the programme now so that a partner is lined up for Stage three.

Decline

The LSHTM track, reopened. Set aside in August and still set aside. The literacy comes from the statistics prerequisite; the network is reachable later by invitation. Reopening it here costs a year and buys nothing the destination needs.

Stage three · The counselling master's

The first stage with tri-council money in it.

Enrolment in a Canadian graduate programme is the eligibility condition for almost every scholarship in the country. Most of these are applied for in the autumn before the year they fund, which means the first applications go in during the first term — before there is anything to show for the degree except the file that got you in.

Confirmed pattern

SSHRC Canada Graduate Scholarships — Master's. The tri-council master's award, applied for through the university in the autumn, funded for twelve months. Eligibility has historically required full-time enrolment, with accommodation for part-time study in some circumstances; check both the current amount and the part-time provisions, because they decide whether this is available to a working student.

Confirmed pattern

The BC Graduate Scholarship and the university's own entrance awards. Provincial graduate scholarships allocated through UBC and SFU, and each school's entrance and dean's awards. Some are nominated by the department at admission, which is one reason the supervisor conversation happens before the application: a department that wants the file nominates the file.

Confirmed pattern

Mitacs Accelerate, with a disability organisation as partner. A paid research internship inside the degree, on a question the partner wants answered — an essential-requirements review for a regulator, an outcome study for a community mental health programme. It funds the thesis and it puts the research where it will be used. Lined up in Stage two; applied for in the first term here.

Verify

Professional association student awards. The Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association and the Canadian Psychological Association both run student awards and student research prizes, and BCACC offers student membership. Small money; real lines on a file. Check what each currently offers and when.

Verify

Grants for students with disabilities, if applicable. Canada Student Grants for students with disabilities and for services and equipment run alongside provincial student aid and are not tied to income in the way loans are. Only relevant if the eligibility applies; worth knowing rather than assuming either way.

Nomination

Research assistantships that become co-authorship. A supervisor whose work touches disability has projects, and projects have papers. An assistantship taken in the first term, on a project near the thesis, is the most reliable route to a second-authored paper before the doctoral application — and it is offered, not applied for, which is what the supervisor conversation was for.

Confirmed pattern

The practicum inside the employer. A placement in the university's own counselling centre or accessibility office, negotiated as a staff member. It is the one arrangement that shallows the trough and builds the record at once, and it is the reason Stage one targets UBC and SFU by name.

Decline

A second master's-level credential in parallel. Anything that is not the degree, the practicum, the assistantship, and the studio Sundays. This is the stage the salary is carrying at a fraction; it is not the stage to add.

Stage four · Art therapy

A small professional community, where a strong file is visible.

Art therapy in Canada is a few hundred registered practitioners and one professional association. That is a disadvantage for anonymity and an advantage for standing: a diploma student with a disability specialism, a counselling registration, and a shown practice is noticed quickly. The doors here are mostly about being asked.

Confirmed pattern

Canadian Art Therapy Association conference and journal. The annual conference accepts student presentations; the Canadian Journal of Art Therapy publishes practitioner research. The diploma's creative project, or the Make Space material, presented once here, is the beginning of the art-therapy standing that the practice and the later supervision of art therapy students both rest on.

Verify

CATA and VATI student awards and bursaries. Small associations often run a student research award or a bursary for the placement year. Check both at application, because the placement year is the shallowest trough and even a small award changes it.

Confirmed pattern

The placement as the first partnership. A 600-hour placement in a post-secondary or community mental health setting with a disability population is where the practice's first referral relationships come from. The site that trains you is the site that refers to you. Choose it for that.

Confirmed pattern

Canada Council Research and Creation, as a professional artist. By this stage the shown record and the catalogue are several years deep, which is the eligibility condition. A research-and-creation grant on the diploma's question funds the studio work that becomes the doctoral portfolio, and it is applied for on a file rather than in a room.

Nomination

The first ask. The doctoral application in Stage five needs referees who can speak to the studio work. The placement supervisor, a CATA colleague, and a studio faculty member met during Stage three are asked here — a year early, with the shown record in hand — whether they would write. The answer shapes which programme is applied to.

Decline

Building the practice large. Every new client at this stage is an hour taken from the placement, the creative project, or the doctoral application. The practice is opened here to exist, not to grow.

Stage five · The doctorate

Where the largest money on the ladder is, and the largest nominations.

A funded doctoral student at a Canadian research university is eligible for more than any other person on this path. Almost all of it is applied for in the first term or nominated for in the first year, and the profile that wins it — leadership record, community engagement, a public body of work, a clear question — is exactly what fifteen years of practice and four stages of preparation have produced. This is the stage the ladder was built for.

Confirmed pattern

SSHRC Doctoral Fellowships and the Canada Graduate Scholarship — Doctoral. Research-creation is an eligible mode. Applied for through the university in the autumn of the first year, on a file the earlier stages have already written: the publications, the shown record, the diploma project, the Mitacs report. The tri-council programmes have been reorganised more than once; confirm the current names, amounts, and whether the application goes through the department or direct.

Nomination

Vanier, or its successor. The flagship doctoral award is nominated by the institution and judged on leadership as well as research. A candidate with a fifteen-year practice, a distress line year, a running community programme, standards committee service, and two clinical registrations is the leadership profile the award was designed for. It is not applied for; it is asked for from the department in the first term, and the nominators file is what makes the ask easy.

Confirmed pattern

SIAT's own funding and partnerships. If the doctoral door is Interactive Arts and Technology, the funding shape changes: NSERC as well as SSHRC may apply to design research, Mitacs partnerships are routine, and accessibility-focused labs carry their own research assistantships. Check which faculty hold accessibility or learning-systems grants, because a supervisor with funding is a funded seat.

Confirmed pattern

The university's own doctoral fellowships. UBC's Four Year Fellowship and Killam Doctoral Scholarships; SFU's graduate fellowships and dean's awards. Some are automatic on admission, some nominated, some applied for. Read the graduate funding page of the school before the application, not after the offer, and make the package part of the entry condition.

Confirmed pattern

Community-engaged scholarship programmes. UBC's Public Scholars Initiative funds doctoral students whose work is public-facing and partnered outside the academy; SFU's Community-Engaged Research Initiative runs fellowships for the same shape of work. A practice-based thesis on the disabled and grieving body, with Make Space and a disability organisation as partners, is what these programmes exist to fund — and they are the most under-applied-for money on this list because most doctoral students do not have a partner. You will.

Confirmed pattern

Teaching assistantships and the graduate teaching certificate. UBC and SFU both run a certificate in university teaching for graduate students. Taken alongside studio assistantships, it turns three years of assisting into a teaching dossier with a credential on it — which is what an adjunct appointment is assessed on and what nothing else on the path produces.

Verify

Mitacs, again, and exhibition and travel funds. Accelerate remains available to doctoral students with a partner; art schools also carry internal exhibition, production, and travel funds for the culminating work. Small, frequent, and mostly unclaimed.

Confirmed pattern

Canada Council Research and Creation, Explore and Create, and the disability arts stream, as a doctoral artist. Professional artist status does not lapse on enrolment. The culminating work is fundable from outside the university as well as inside it, and a Canada Council grant on a doctoral thesis is a line the adjunct appointment reads.

Nomination

Sessional teaching in the department before the defence. Doctoral students in art schools are often offered a course in their final years. It is offered to the student whose assistantships were noticed. This is the appointment ladder starting a year before the degree, and it is the reason Stage six's first item is not a cold application.

Decline

Any consulting above the named cap. The most expensive thing that can happen in this stage is a funded year lost to the project that carried it. The cap was set in Stage one for this reason.

Stage six · The portfolio

Seats that are held by people who were nominated for them.

This is the shape the whole ladder was aimed at, and every item in it is reachable from Grace's profile by analogy. None of these is a post you apply for from the outside. They are the seats that a decade inside the sector, a shown record, two registrations, and a doctorate make you the obvious person for — provided someone thinks of you.

Confirmed pattern

Adjunct appointment in an art department. Adjunct and affiliate appointments are nominated by a department for people with a primary career elsewhere who contribute teaching or supervision. The file is the teaching dossier, the shown record, the doctorate, and the two registrations. It is asked for by the department that already knows you from assistantships and the sessional course — which is why those came first.

Confirmed pattern

University equity and accessibility leadership. The primary career Grace held alongside her clinical and academic work was in the university's equity, diversity, and academic-leadership offices. An accessibility advisor with a doctorate, two registrations, and a decade of standing is the internal candidate for the senior access and equity posts — director-level seats that decide institutional policy on essential requirements for every programme at once. This is the highest-leverage seat on the ladder, and the plan's salaried spine leads to it.

Confirmed pattern

Regulator and association committees. The College of Health and Care Professionals' registrant committees, CATA and BCACC boards and standards committees, and the accessibility standards seats begun in Stage one. These are where scope, registration standards, and accessibility requirements are written. A registrant with a disability specialism is asked to sit on them, and should say yes to one at a time.

Nomination

Clinical supervision of counselling and art therapy students. Both registrations permit supervising practicum students, and both programmes need supervisors with the right specialism. It is the teaching stream in the clinical world, it is paid, and it is asked for rather than applied for.

Verify

Postdoctoral fellowships, if the lectern wants one. SSHRC and Banting postdoctoral fellowships, and university-specific postdocs, exist for a two-year research post after the doctorate. The plan does not need one; a fractional academic career in Grace's shape does not require it. Named so that it is a decision rather than an omission.

Verify

Early-career scholarly recognition by nomination. National academies and professional bodies run early-career recognition schemes for scholars within a set number of years of the doctorate, all by nomination. Whether any fit a practice-based doctorate and a fractional career should be checked at the time; the point is that the nominators file is still in use twenty years after it was started.

Confirmed pattern

The studio's own ladder. Exhibitions, public art commissions, Canada Council and BC Arts Council grants at mid-career levels, and the Foundations teaching module as its own income line. The stream that was held at a floor for two decades in earlier plans is, here, a career in its own right — which is what it was always meant to be.

Strengths

What the profile makes easy, and what it has to build.

The CliftonStrengths ranking is a working tool here, not a horoscope. Ten themes at the top — Intellection, Learner, Input, Restorative, Strategic, Individualization, Achiever, Responsibility, Futuristic, Discipline — and four at the bottom — Woo, Command, Context, Competition — describe a person who wins on written files, long preparation, and one-to-one work, and who does not naturally do the thing most of the nominated items on this ladder require: being thought of.

What is already an advantage

Every application on this ladder is a written case built from a deep record. Intellection, Learner and Input are the themes that produce the record; Strategic and Futuristic are the themes that wrote this plan; Discipline and Achiever are why the studio-day standing order will actually hold. Restorative and Individualization are the clinical themes, and they are why the practice will be good rather than merely registered. This profile is built for the file.

What has to be built deliberately

Nomination requires being visible to the people who nominate, and Woo, Significance and Communication sit low. The compensating mechanism is written and scheduled rather than social: the nominators file, sent the shown record twice a year; one presentation a year at the venue nearest the next application; a public writing rhythm that does the introducing so that no room has to. Grace's career reads as being asked; the asking was preceded by a record that made the asking easy.

The failure mode to watch

Deliberative at thirteen and Activator at twenty-three means the risk is not rashness but readiness that never becomes an application. Every item above has a date on it or a stage in which it is asked for. The rule: an opportunity that is being prepared for and has no date written next to it is not being prepared for.

The 80,000 Hours test, applied to the person

Personal fit is the multiplier the framework says matters most, and this profile fits three of the ladder's seats unusually well: standards and policy work, which rewards Strategic and Input; the clinical room, which rewards Restorative and Individualization; and a practice-based doctorate, which rewards Intellection and Discipline. It fits the promotional side of an academic career least. The plan's answer is to hold that side at the minimum the nominations require, and to let the work do the introducing.

None of this is visible from the programme pages, and all of it is reachable from a record that is being kept anyway. The ladder is climbed by the file, and the file is being written now.
