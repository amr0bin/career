## 1. Three findings that change the picture

1. **Security arrives at Stage one, not Stage six.** The UBC Accessibility Advisor role was posted in spring 2026 at $8,305 to $12,952 a month, about $99,700 to $155,400 a year, with a pension. UBC normally hires between the minimum and the midpoint, which is about $127,500. SFU's Disability Access Advisor sits at Grade 11, $98,297 to $117,276 on the July 2026 scale, with a pension the university funds entirely. The first role on this path is already a six-figure, pensioned career. Everything after it adds range and choice.
2. **Every later stage has funding that can be named.** The master's and the doctorate were carried in earlier files as an unfunded trough. The figures in section 3 show a tuition waiver, a $27,000 master's scholarship, and a $40,000-a-year doctoral minimum at UBC.
3. **In BC the psychologist title runs through one counselling psychology door.** Registration as a psychologist requires a doctorate. UBC's PhD in Counselling Psychology is CPA-accredited and admits only from a thesis-based master's. SFU has no counselling psychology doctorate. The thesis-based master's decision in `05` is therefore confirmed as load-bearing.

## 2. The ceiling, and what is past it

**The ceiling already written:** optionality. Work chosen across the room, the lectern and the studio, with a joined access-and-mental-health centre as the institutional form of the thesis.

**Past it.** Each of these becomes reachable because of one fact: a registered psychologist in BC may diagnose, and UBC accepts learning disability documentation only from a registered psychologist or certified school psychologist. A person who holds that authority and also knows accommodation law, essential requirements and disability studies stands on both sides of the gate that decides who gets access.

| Beyond the ceiling | What makes it possible |
|---|---|
| **Founding director of a joined access and mental health centre** at UBC or SFU | The master's thesis as the evidence base; a decade inside an accessibility unit; the doctorate |
| **Redesigning the documentation gate itself**: an accessible, lower-cost assessment service, and a published standard for what documentation should and should not be asked for | Diagnostic authority plus the accommodation thesis. Private psychoeducational assessments in the Lower Mainland run about $3,300 to $3,900 each, which is itself an access barrier |
| **A national standards voice** on essential requirements and clinical placement access | The corpus study, a technical committee seat, the Leeds MSc |
| **Professor of Teaching, or a clinical-academic appointment** | The doctorate, the teaching dossier, the published method |
| **A licensed training system** that others run | Accessible Learning Labs' method applied to Creative Health Studio's programme |
| **An art practice funded on juried competition** | The catalogue, the showings, two curated exhibitions |

**Fallback, named once:** if the doctorate is not taken, RCC registration plus a continuing advisor or leadership role inside a university is a complete, secure career in its own right.

## 4. What turns the ceiling into a starting point

Seven enablers, in order of leverage. Each is something she controls.

1. **Get inside a university, as a continuing employee.** This single move delivers the salary band, the pension, the tuition waiver, the internal-candidate route and the committee seat. "Continuing" matters: SFU's waiver and most benefits attach to continuing appointments, so a term role is the way in and conversion is the target.
2. **Choose the employer with Stage three in mind.** An SFU continuing employee taking the SFU master's has tuition waived, and SFU schedules classes in the late afternoon and evening. A UBC employee gets the prerequisites free (12 undergraduate credits a year) and a partial master's waiver, and UBC is where the accredited doctorate is. Either works. Price the difference at the offer stage.
3. **Treat prerequisite grades as scholarship evidence.** The master's scholarship and the doctoral awards are adjudicated on academic record and research potential. UBC's minimum is a B+ average. Competitive files sit well above it.
4. **Start the helping-experience clock now.** UBC's department page asks for two years or 450 hours; its Calendar says three years. Crisis Centre BC is the clock. Planning to the three-year figure keeps the December 2030 application safe, and an early start opens the option of applying a year sooner.
5. **Have a supervisor who wants the question before applying.** SFU admits at most 12 from 250 to 300 applicants. UBC's MA expects research experience and the GRE. Faculty fit decides files at this level.
6. **Publish before each gate.** The four resources, the mini-corpus piece, the conceptual paper and the scoping review are the record every panel in section 3 reads.
7. **Build one asset that earns without her presence.** A licensed training system or course. It is the mechanism that lets the caseload stay small and the studio stay protected.

**On strengths.** The 2015 Gallup profile leads with Intellection, Learner, Input, Restorative and Strategic. Those are the working habits of a scientist-practitioner and of someone who reasons through accommodation decisions. They also say how she becomes known: through written work and one-to-one conversations, which is how every gate on this path reads a candidate.

## 7. The 80,000 Hours lens, applied honestly

80,000 Hours ranks problems by scale, neglectedness and solvability, adds personal fit, and treats early stages as building career capital: skills, connections, credentials, character and runway.

- **Scale.** In the 2025 Canadian University Survey Consortium survey, 33% of first-year students reported a disability, and 17% a mental health condition. WHO has called depression the leading cause of ill health and disability worldwide (2017 release; its current fact sheet gives about 322 million people).
- **Neglectedness.** No local researcher holds this question (section 6). The scoping review tests the claim properly.
- **Solvability.** The levers are documentation rules, wait times and interim measures, which universities control and can change for every student at once.
- **Personal fit.** A decade of applied accessibility and learning design, the Leeds degree, a role inside the system being studied, and later diagnostic authority.
- **Runway is career capital.** The framework itself lists it. Stability is part of the impact case, which is the argument `the-strategy-layer.md` already makes.

**The limit, stated plainly.** 80,000 Hours rates mental health as "sometimes recommended" and has no disability profile, and its advising service prioritises other problems. The lens is used here as a method. The case for this path rests on fit and on leverage, and it should be re-run when the scoping review reports.
