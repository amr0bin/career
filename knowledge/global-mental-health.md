# Global mental health

> **Purpose:** Why global mental health belongs in this arc, what task-shifting teaches the work, and the five-rung credential ladder with its decision rules. · **Governs:** whether and when each GMH credential is taken. · **Carried out in Notion:** {{notion:Gates}}

## The ceiling

Global mental health is the field that sets the agenda for how depression care scales. At the top of the arc there are advisory, research and policy roles there, and they carry the **disability question** into a field that has largely studied scale without studying access. The combination is rare: a disability studies master's, a counselling registration, a PhD on whether the accommodation process drives depression, and a formal GMH line. Very few researchers hold it. GMH also supplies the model the whole practice extends: **task-shifting**, where trained non-specialists deliver simplified, effective depression care at scale. Make Space, the facilitator manual and the Creative Health peer programme are that model with creative approaches added.

## What task-shifting teaches the work

The primary evidence comes from the trials, not from the overview PDFs. (The two STORM task-shifting overviews are maps: generic, LMIC-framed, uncited. See `bodies-of-knowledge.md`, k6.)

- **It works, with the right structure.** Lay counsellors in Goa (MANAS, 2010) and lay health workers on the Friendship Bench in Zimbabwe (2016) reduced common mental disorders in randomised trials. The Friendship Bench began in 2006 with fourteen grandmothers delivering a problem-solving therapy developed in Shona and grounded in local concepts. It was built outward because patients could not reach the clinic.
- **The intervention is only half of it. The training system is the other half.** Every scaled programme depends on simplified protocols, competency-based training, **supportive supervision** and fidelity checks. That is learning design applied to care, and it is where instructional design, practised over the past decade, becomes a clinical asset (k8).
- **Cultural and contextual adaptation is part of the method, not a courtesy.** Programmes that held were built in local language and concepts.
- **Scale fails on implementation, not efficacy.** Sustainability needs political commitment, ongoing training and community ownership. Use CFIR, RE-AIM, Proctor's outcomes and hybrid designs to plan it, and KTA to carry it (k6).
- **The access question it leaves open.** The task-shifting literature asks how to reach more people. It rarely asks which disabled people its delivery formats exclude: bench, clinic, group, phone. That gap is where this work contributes.

**What it means for Make Space.** Place it on the IASC intervention pyramid (community and family supports, focused non-specialised supports). Manualise it. Train facilitators so it runs in rooms she is not in. Measure it with a validated screen. That is how a practice becomes a programme that is licensable and fundable.

## Decision rules

1. **Rung 2 is worth taking regardless.** It is the first formal GMH line, and its policy report feeds the Leeds essays and the MA statement.
2. **Rung 3 is taken only if, by summer 2029, the thesis question or a funder (CIHR, Mitacs) is framed in global or policy terms.** Otherwise the money and hours go to the MA preparation.
3. **Rungs 4 and 5 are never taken at the same time as the MA practicum year or the PhD internship.**
4. **The three-year transfer clock on GHM202 is the only deadline in the track.**

**A promotion question to pair with rule 2:** what would it take for the GHM202 policy report on depression and disability to be published as a policy brief, or to become the annual depression brief? That turns a module assessment into a public output.

**Closed or impractical (verified 14 October 2026):** the Glasgow online GMH programme (no longer accepting applications), King's/LSHTM MSc Global Mental Health (campus, full-time), and Edinburgh MSc GMH and Society (campus, full-time). No McGill or Toronto online GMH certificate was found.

## Barriers, as tasks

- **Hours during Leeds.** GHM202 runs October to June, overlapping Leeds modules. Task: confirm Leeds module-break rules before applying, and place GHM202 in the year with the lighter prerequisite load.
- **Currency and fees.** These are in GBP and change annually. Task: re-check each spring and log the figure in Notion. Ask whether an employer professional-development fund or the tuition waiver covers external modules.
- **Relevance to the counselling file.** Verify whether GHM202 or a PgCert counts toward the MA's research-experience line or toward CPACC recertification.

**Fallback, once:** if no formal rung beyond rung 1 fits before the MA, the GMH line is carried by reading (k6) and the Make Space facilitator manual until rung 5 becomes available after RCC.
