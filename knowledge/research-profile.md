## The line, in one sentence

How university accommodation processes act as disabling barriers in their own right, and which of their design features shape disabled students' wellbeing and help-seeking — studied through the social model and psycho-emotional disablism, and aimed at redesigns institutions can make.

Every output below is a piece of that sentence. A reader who meets any one of them should be able to guess the others.

## What the profile has to show by 1 Dec 2030

The MA application and the CGRS M proposal are read by people who see many promising files. The profile that stands out shows:

- **Sustained inquiry:** the same question, deepening, across five years of dated outputs.
- **Completion:** at least two pieces through peer review or preprint, not only "in progress".
- **Method range:** conceptual (1), evidence synthesis (3), document analysis (5) and an empirical pilot (7).
- **Partnership:** a disabled people's organisation or student organisation that has worked with you, and a co-researcher who is a co-author.
- **A supervisor who has already seen the work**, from the bridge years in `arc/the-thesis-line.md`.

## Acceleration levers

- **Turn every assessed piece into a public piece.** A marked essay is already reviewed once; a month of revision turns it into a preprint or resource.
- **Publish the evidence map independently, before Leeds starts using it.** Leeds work then cites it as prior work, rather than repeating it.
- **Plan the pilot paper's structure into the Applied Project report** so the report and the paper share a skeleton.
- **Co-author with the co-researcher and partner** from the design stage, so authorship is agreed early and the paper is not delayed by it.
