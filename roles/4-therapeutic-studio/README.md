# Role 4 · Owner-Practitioner, Therapeutic Studio

> **When:** opens small at RCC scope (~2034) with a sliding scale from the first client; art therapy integrated after the VATI diploma and RCAT registration; psychologist scope after registration (~2040–41). Held at a deliberate size alongside the institutional role. **Three parts, kept separate on purpose:** (a) the **clinical practice** (counselling, then psychology, with art therapy modalities); (b) the **Creative Health peer programme**, with Make Space inside it, run by trained peer facilitators; (c) the **business** that holds both.

## The ceiling for this role

A small, excellent practice specialising in depression and fluctuating conditions for disabled adults and students, with art-based methods used where they help; a Creative Health peer programme that runs without you because it is manualised and its facilitators are trained and supervised; and a business that is legally clean, financially steady and sized by desire, not need (`../../philosophy/excellence-and-working-rules.md`).

## Competencies

**Frameworks:** BCACC *Entry-to-Practice Competency Profile* and *Standards of Clinical Practice* (then CHCPBC standards once psychotherapy is regulated from 29 November 2027, and CHCPBC psychology standards at psychologist scope); Canadian Art Therapy Association *Standards of Practice* and *Ethical Guidelines*; Peer Support Canada *Standards of Practice* and *Code of Conduct*; BC *Personal Information Protection Act* (PIPA).

## Systems

| Rhythm | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Per client session** | Note the same day; measure if used; artwork logged per consent |
| **Weekly** | Practice admin block (billing, receipts, scheduling); peer facilitator check-in; clinical consultation |
| **Monthly** | Finances against budget; sliding-scale capacity; waitlist; peer programme attendance and feedback (aggregate) |
| **Quarterly** | Policy review (privacy, consent, fees); outcomes review; facilitator supervision group |
| **Yearly** | Insurance; registration renewals; CE; accessibility audit of the practice; programme evaluation; the consulting test (would I take this if I didn't need the money?) |

**Two ledgers, never mixed:** clinical records in the practice-management system; peer programme records (attendance, facilitator logs, evaluation) in a separate system with its own consent. Nothing from either appears in public writing.

## Tools

A Canadian practice-management and clinical-records platform with Canadian data storage (Jane and Owl Practice are widely used in BC; check data residency and BCACC guidance) · secure video · accounting software · a separate peer-programme registration tool · the studio space set up for art materials safety and storage.

## Getting in (opening), and the first 90 days

- **Before opening:** registration held; insurance; the launch checklist complete; the naming question under the protected title settled (`../../philosophy/house-rules.md`, open item).
- **Days 1–30:** five clients at most; every policy tested on real flow; consultation weekly.
- **31–90:** decide the sustainable caseload; open the sliding-scale slots; start the peer programme's next cohort under trained facilitators.

## Boundaries

- **The peer register and the clinical offer never share a page** (`../../arc/throughlines.md`). Peer facilitators don't counsel; the practitioner doesn't run peer groups as therapy.
- **Before registration, no therapy is offered in any form** (house rule 1), and no "therapist" language in advertising.
- **No dual relationships** with students advised or counselled in the institutional role.
- **The private nature practice is not part of the business.**

## What it hands forward

Practice income through the doctorate; a manualised peer programme ready to be studied or scaled; referral partnerships (the VATI placement site first); the Creative Health candidate intervention for the PhD.
