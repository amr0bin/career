# Practice Launch Checklist

> Template · Before the first client · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

## Regulatory
- [ ] Registration current (RCC; later RCAT and psychologist); titles used correctly in every listing
- [ ] Advertising reviewed against BCACC and CHCPBC standards; naming question under the protected title settled
- [ ] Professional liability insurance (and general liability for the space)

## Legal and business
- [ ] Business structure chosen with an accountant; business licence for the location
- [ ] GST position confirmed (counselling services may be exempt depending on the provider's registration; confirm with the CRA or an accountant)
- [ ] Bank account and bookkeeping system

## Clinical systems
- [ ] Practice-management platform with Canadian data storage; privacy assessment done
- [ ] Informed consent, privacy policy, fee policy published
- [ ] Record retention and secure destruction procedure
- [ ] Emergency and risk procedures; referral list; consultation or supervision arranged
- [ ] Telehealth policy (client location and jurisdiction)

## Access
- [ ] Physical, digital, financial and procedural access audited (learning-design 08 checklist)
- [ ] Sliding-scale slots defined

## Art therapy (after RCAT)
- [ ] Materials safety, ventilation, storage; artwork consent and retention policy
