# Peer Programme Group Agreement

> Template · First session of every Creative Health group · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

This is a **peer support** group, not therapy. Facilitators are trained peers who share lived experience; they do not diagnose, treat or give clinical advice.

Together we agree to:
- Keep what is shared here in the group
- Speak from our own experience
- Respect different ways of taking part, including making without sharing
- Make access needs known at any time, without explanation
- Look after ourselves: step out whenever you need to

If someone needs more than the group can offer, the facilitator will talk with them privately and share resources (9-8-8; local crisis lines; counselling options).

Standards: Peer Support Canada *Standards of Practice* and *Code of Conduct*.
