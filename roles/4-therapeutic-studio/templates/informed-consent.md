# Informed Consent and Service Agreement

> Template · Every new client, before the first session · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

- Who I am, my registration and what services I provide (and do not)
- How sessions work: length, frequency, in person or video, cancellation
- Fees, sliding scale, receipts for extended health plans
- Confidentiality and its limits (risk of serious harm, child protection, court order, as law requires)
- Records: what I keep, how, for how long, and your right to access
- Use of art materials and artwork (see the artwork consent)
- Measures I may ask you to complete, and why
- Electronic communication and its risks
- What to do in an emergency (9-8-8, 9-1-1); I am not an emergency service
- Ending: you can stop at any time; how we will close
- Complaints: how to raise a concern with me, and with the regulator

**Signature and date**
