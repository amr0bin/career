# Peer Referral Thresholds

> Template · When peer support is not enough · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

| Situation | Facilitator action |
|---|---|
| Immediate risk of harm to self or others | Stay with the person if safe; call 9-1-1 or 9-8-8; tell the programme lead the same day |
| Distress beyond what the group can hold | Private check-in; offer resources; suggest clinical support; follow up per policy |
| Disclosure that may require reporting (e.g. a child at risk) | Follow the reporting procedure; contact the programme lead |
| Repeated need for one-to-one support | Discuss with the programme lead; refer to counselling options |

Facilitators never refer group members to the practice's own clinical services without the programme lead's review (avoids dual-relationship pressure).
