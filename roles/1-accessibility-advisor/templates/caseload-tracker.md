# Caseload Tracker

> Template · Daily and weekly (in the institutional system) · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

Fields to keep in the unit's case-management system (never in a personal spreadsheet with real names):

| Case ID | Opened | Status | Accommodations | Interim measures | Next review date | Pending item | Last contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Weekly: sort by next review date; close loops on pending items older than one week.
