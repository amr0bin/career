# Documentation Review Checklist

> Template · Is the documentation enough — and no more? · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

- [ ] From a qualified professional, with the date
- [ ] Describes **functional impact** in an academic setting (diagnosis is not required where policy allows)
- [ ] Indicates expected duration or fluctuation
- [ ] Supports the specific accommodations requested
- [ ] Nothing beyond what the decision needs has been requested or kept
- [ ] If incomplete: interim measures put in place, and exactly what is still needed stated in plain language

Reference: OHRC *Policy on Accessible Education for Students with Disabilities* (2018); AHEAD documentation guidance; UBC LR7 or SFU GP 26.
