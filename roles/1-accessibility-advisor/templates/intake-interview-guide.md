# Intake Interview Guide

> Template · First meeting with a student · Blank form: copy into the institution's or practice's own system. Never store a real student's or client's information in this repository.

## Opening (5 min)
- Welcome; how long we have; what this meeting is for
- Confidentiality: what stays here, what instructors see (the accommodation, never the diagnosis), the limits

## The student's account (15 min)
- "Tell me about your studies and where things get harder."
- Which settings: lectures, labs, exams, placements, online, deadlines
- Pattern over time: steady, episodic, fluctuating? What changes it?
- What has helped before, here or elsewhere?

## Functional impact (10 min)
- What the barrier does in this environment (describe the interaction, not the condition)
- What the documentation shows about functional impact; what is missing, if anything

## Options and next steps (10 min)
- Accommodations that address the barrier; interim measures if documentation is pending
- Review date; how to reach me if things change
- Referrals offered (with the student's agreement)

## After the meeting
- Note written the same day in the case-management system
- Letter issued within the service standard
