# Role 3 · Adjunct Professor, Sessional and Teaching-Stream Lecturer

> **When:** after the PhD (~2040 →), alongside psychologist practice. **What the title means at UBC:** adjunct professors hold "part-time appointments granted to individuals who have some special professional skill" and who are "primarily engaged in the practice of their professions outside UBC or any other academic institution" (UBC HR, faculty titles). Adjunct appointments are often unpaid or honorary and come with teaching, supervision or research collaboration; paid course teaching is usually a **sessional lecturer** appointment. Confirm terms at each institution (UBC, SFU, UVic, VATI). **Where:** counselling psychology (UBC ECPS; SFU Education; UVic), art therapy programmes, disability studies.

## The ceiling for this role

The lectern as a multiplier: teaching the courses that train the next counsellors and advisors to design access in from the start; supervising theses and practicum students; a research collaboration that keeps the joined access-and-mental-health work going; and, in time, a research chair nomination from the record. At full height it can also be a **teaching-stream** career: Lecturer, then the Professor of Teaching ranks, leading how a department teaches disability, inclusion and creative health.

## Benchmark posting · UBC Psychology Lecturer (read 5 Nov 2026)

UBC Department of Psychology, **Lecturer**, Faculty of Arts: full-time, **24 credits a year**, initial three-year term including a probationary year, renewable; **$7,500–$8,000 a month** (about $90,000–$96,000 a year) with UBC benefits; Canadians and permanent residents given priority. Requires a **PhD in psychology or a related discipline at hire** (counselling psychology: **verify** item 56). Prefers broad knowledge plus depth in a subdiscipline the department holds; experience developing and delivering university courses; varied teaching techniques; willingness to teach **intro psychology, intro quantitative methods** and upper-level courses. Application: letter with three referees, CV, teaching philosophy, one-page equity statement, evidence of teaching effectiveness. Deadline early January for a July start.

**Earliest clean match:** the final PhD year (~Jan 2040), with the defence before July. Reasoning: `../../decisions/2026-11-05-ubc-psychology-lecturer-benchmark.md`.

## Competencies

**Frameworks:** CPA *Ethical Guidelines for Supervision in Psychology: Teaching, Research, Practice, and Administration* (2017); CPA accreditation standards for doctoral programmes (what faculty contribute); the teaching dossier conventions used by UBC CTLT; UDL Guidelines 3.0.

| Competency | What good looks like | How to build it | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Course design** | Aligned outcomes, assessments and activities; UDL built in; an access statement that means it | Learning-design kit (06, 05, 08) | Syllabi; course evaluations |
| **Teaching and facilitation** | Active learning; inclusive classroom; good feedback | TA and guest lectures from the MA on | Peer observations |
| **Assessment and grading** | Rubrics shared in advance; timely, forward-looking feedback; integrity by design | Learning-design 05 | Rubrics; feedback samples |
| **Clinical teaching and supervision** | Supervises practicum students to CPA guidelines; evaluates competence fairly | Supervision training; supervised supervision | Supervisee evaluations |
| **Graduate supervision** | Clear expectations, regular meetings, timely feedback, authorship agreed early | The thesis method (`../../knowledge/how-to-write-a-thesis.md` step 8, from the other side) | Completed theses |
| **Research collaboration** | Funded or co-funded projects; co-authorship with students | Postdoc; grants | Publications; grants |
| **Academic service** | Committees, peer review, community engagement | Reviewer roles from the first publication | CV service section |
| **Professional boundaries** | Separates teaching, supervision and therapy; no clients as students | CPA code and guidelines | — |

## Getting in, and the first 90 days

- **Build the dossier from now.** Every TA role, guest lecture and workshop goes in, with a peer observation and short student feedback (`../../structures/experiences.html`). The plan, the past workshops and the four anticipatory courses: [`teaching-dossier.md`](teaching-dossier.md).
- **Route in:** sessional teaching in the department where you trained; an adjunct appointment usually follows an existing working relationship (practicum supervision, committee work, research collaboration).
- **First term:** teach a course you already know (or co-teach); ask for a peer observation in week 4; collect mid-term feedback.

## Boundaries

- **No current clients as students**, and no students as clients.
- **Supervision is not therapy**, even when personal material arises; refer.
- **Teaching examples are composites or published cases**, never derived from real clients (house rule 2).

## What it hands forward

Supervisees who become colleagues and referral partners; the research collaboration behind the joined centre; standing for director-level and standards roles in Optionality.
