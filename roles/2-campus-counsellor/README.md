# Role 2 · Campus Counsellor (RCC), then Registered Psychologist — UBC, SFU or UVic

> **When:** practicum from ~2033; counsellor role on conferral (~2034) with RCC (BCACC); psychologist scope after the PhD, internship and CHCPBC registration (~2040–41). **Where:** UBC Counselling Services; SFU Health & Counselling; UVic Counselling Services. **Specialism:** depression, and fluctuating or episodic conditions, with disabled students first.

## The ceiling for this role

A counsellor who is the person campus services send the students others find hard to help: disabled students, students whose mood flattens mid-term, students the stepped-care model misses. Then the psychologist who builds the joined access-and-mental-health intake the dissertation tests, and who supervises the next generation.

## Competencies

**Frameworks:** BCACC *Entry-to-Practice Competency Profile for RCCs* (approved January 2023, in effect November 2023; aligned with the Ontario CRPO and Nova Scotia frameworks, adding digital practice, EDI, and Indigenous cultural safety, cultural humility and anti-racism); BCACC *Code of Ethical Conduct* and *Standards of Clinical Practice*; CPA *Canadian Code of Ethics for Psychologists* (4th ed., 2017) and CPA accreditation competencies at psychologist level; CSA **Z2003:20** *Mental Health and Well-Being for Post-Secondary Students* for the campus system.

## Systems

| Rhythm | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Per session** | Note within the day (DAP); measures scored and discussed; risk reviewed if indicated |
| **Weekly** | Clinical supervision or peer consultation; caseload and wait-list review; reflection entry |
| **Monthly** | Outcome review across the caseload (aggregate); one training hour; review of any open risk cases |
| **Yearly** | Continuing education to the BCACC (and later CHCPBC) requirement; insurance; registration renewal; one conference (CCPA, CPA) |

**Records:** the service's electronic record only. Supervision notes and reflections are anonymised and kept in Notion Private.

## Tools

The centre's electronic clinical record and scheduling system (learn at hire) · secure video platform approved by the institution · routine outcome measures (PHQ-9, GAD-7; CCAPS where the centre uses it; ORS/SRS for brief work) · safety-plan template · referral directory (campus and community, including 9-8-8 and the BC crisis lines).

## Getting in, and the first 90 days

- **The practicum is the interview.** UBC Counselling Services takes a small number of master's practicum students a year; the application needs a mock-session recording, references and an interview (`../../arc/stage-2-the-room.md`). Treat it as a years-long interview.
- **Apply the week of conferral**, with RCC submitted and CCC eligibility documented.
- **Days 1–30:** the centre's protocols (risk, after-hours, mandatory reporting), the record system, the referral map. **31–60:** a full caseload with weekly supervision. **61–90:** propose or co-facilitate one group for a population the centre under-serves (for example, students with fluctuating conditions).

## Boundaries

- **Nothing client-derived, ever**, in any public piece, paper or teaching example (house rule 2).
- **No dual relationships:** no students you advised as an accessibility advisor become clients without discussion with your supervisor; no clients join Make Space or the peer programme.
- **Research separation:** never study your own clients; the dissertation recruits through partners and ethics approval.
- **Titles:** use only the titles you hold (RCC; "psychologist" only after registration).

## What it hands forward

Clinical standing in the dissertation's own setting; internal candidacy for a CPA-accredited internship at a campus centre; clinical referees; the practice income that runs through the doctorate; the case for the joined intake.
