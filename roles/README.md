## Every kit has the same anatomy

1. **The ceiling for the role**: what holding it at full height looks like.
2. **Competencies**: a recognised framework, then a table of what to build, how to evidence it, and by when.
3. **Systems**: the weekly and monthly rhythms, records and reviews that run the role.
4. **Tools**: what to learn, and what to check before using.
5. **Templates**: blank, reusable forms in the kit's `templates/` folder.
6. **Getting in, and the first 90 days.**
7. **Boundaries**: what the role must never mix with the others.
8. **What it hands forward** to the next role.

## Competencies that carry across every role

Building these once pays out five times.

| Cross-role competency | Learning designer | Advisor | Counsellor / psychologist | Adjunct professor | Therapeutic Studio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Construct vs method** (what is being measured vs one way of showing it) | Assessment design | Essential requirements, *Meiorin* | Assessment, formulation that locates barriers | Course and assessment design | Programme design that needs no accommodation |
| **Intake and needs assessment** | Stage 3 and 6 | Intake interview | Clinical intake and risk screen | Thesis supervision start-up | Client intake; peer programme registration |
| **Documentation that is minimal, accurate and defensible** | Decision log | Determination memo | Clinical records | Grading and supervision records | Records under PIPA; programme records |
| **Facilitation** | Workshops | Faculty consultations and training | Group counselling | Seminars | Peer groups; art therapy groups |
| **Evaluation** | Kirkpatrick, QA | Service quality assurance | Measurement-based care | Course evaluation | Outcome monitoring; programme evaluation |
| **Ethics and boundaries** | IP and AI disclosure | Confidentiality, FIPPA | Codes of ethics, duty to report, dual relationships | Supervision ethics | Advertising, titles, peer vs clinical separation |
| **Equity and cultural safety** | UDL 3.0 | Human rights law | Indigenous cultural safety, cultural humility and anti-racism | Inclusive teaching | All of these, owned |

## Shared systems across all roles

- **One reflection log** from day one in each role (helping hours count; private, anonymised at the point of writing).
- **One supervision and consultation log** (Notion Private).
- **One evidence portfolio**: three case studies per role, never with real clients, students or accommodation cases (house rules 2 and 5).
- **One quarterly review**: is each role being done at its ceiling, and is any one crowding the others? (`../philosophy/ledgers-and-review.md`)
