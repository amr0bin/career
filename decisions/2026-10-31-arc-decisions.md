# 2026-10-31 · Three decisions that set the arc

> **Purpose:** the reasoning behind the three choices that turned six-stage plans into one arc. · **Governs:** any older page that disagrees on the doctorate, the MA year, or art therapy. · **Carried out in Notion:** {{notion:Decisions}}

## Context

The planning files had drifted into two versions. Three files (`career.html`, `my-vision.html`, `10-stability-and-the-ceiling.md`, all late Sept–Oct 2026) planned a counselling psychology doctorate. Five older pages (`the-sequence`, `timeline`, `checklist`, `deployment`, `twenty-years`) planned an art doctorate instead. Separately, `education-path` and `therapeutic-practice` disagreed on the counselling MA entry year by twelve months, and art therapy was placed inconsistently or not at all. Nothing downstream could be dated until these were settled.

## Decision 1 · The doctorate is the UBC PhD in Counselling Psychology

- **Why:** it is the only route in BC to the psychologist title, it is the credential that leads service and holds research chairs, and it carries the thesis question (does the accommodation process itself drive depression in disabled students?) to its full depth.
- **What it closes:** the art doctorate (SFU Contemporary Arts, UBC Art History, SIAT) is recorded as considered and set aside in `options/doctoral-routes.md`. The art practice continues through Studio, art therapy and Creative Health; it is not lost, it is routed.
- **What would reopen it:** see `options/doctoral-routes.md`.

## Decision 2 · Counselling MA entry is September 2031

- **Why:** the Leeds MSc runs Sept 2027 to about Aug 2029. A 2031 entry leaves a clean year after Leeds to finish prerequisites, build supervised helping experience and warm referees, instead of stacking the MA application on top of the Leeds dissertation. The Accessibility Advisor role through that period is both the income and the staff tuition waiver that funds the prerequisites.
- **What it fixes:** all counselling prerequisites graded by **31 Dec 2030**; UBC and SFU applications by **1 Dec 2030** in the same cycle; practicum around Jan 2033; RCC around 2033–34.
- **Superseded:** `education-path`'s Sept 2030 entry and its 2029 application dates.

## Decision 3 · Art therapy (VATI Advanced Diploma) comes after the MA, before the PhD

- **Why:** every source agrees the VATI Advanced Diploma requires a completed counselling master's, so it cannot run alongside the MA. Placing it before the PhD, alongside early RCC practice, means art therapy becomes part of the practice the doctorate studies, rather than an add-on during the most demanding years.
- **What it fixes:** VATI around 2034–35; PhD start around Sept 2035.
