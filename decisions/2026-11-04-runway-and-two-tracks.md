# 2026-11-04 · The two tracks, and the Applied Project runway

> **Purpose:** the reasoning behind adding a deliberate "game track" to the plan and starting the Applied Project two years before its module opens. · **Governs:** `philosophy/the-two-tracks.md`, `knowledge/research-profile.md`, `arc/applied-project-runway.md`. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Decisions](https://app.notion.com/p/3cdae687f6934e57a7e1d3510e2cf898)

## Decision 1 · Train the game track on purpose

- **What:** every gate now carries *Unwritten criteria*, *Who to ask* and an *Earliest credible date*; every meeting gets a written summary sent back the same day and logged; every output is planned before it starts; a quarterly acceleration review asks both how a gate could slip and what it would take to arrive early.
- **Why:** excellent work and knowing how institutions decide are separate skills. Careers that move fast tend to aim every piece of work at the next position's real criteria. The repo already served the craft track well; the game track was implicit.
- **Source of the idea:** a public account (4 Nov 2026) by a professor who went from midwife to full professor, with tenure in one year, crediting written meeting records, learning how careers are built, strategic publishing and funding, and deliberate choices.

## Decision 2 · Start the Applied Project now, inside the rules

- **What:** from Nov 2026, build everything the project needs that does not involve participants: the evidence map, protocol, instrument draft, measures, partner, advisory group, funding, data management plan, ethics draft and report skeleton. Participant data waits for Leeds ethics approval.
- **Why:** the module (OSSP5300M) gives six guided weeks to propose, review, prepare ethics and plan, then about four and a half months to run and report. Arriving with that work done turns the module into execution, gives the report the best chance of excellence, and makes a pilot paper possible before 1 Dec 2030.
- **Stretch:** ask Leeds whether the nine taught modules can be completed by August 2028, so the project runs September 2028–February 2029.
