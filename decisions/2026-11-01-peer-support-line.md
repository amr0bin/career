# 2026-11-01 · The peer support line: certified peer supporter, then mentor

> **Purpose:** the reasoning for adding national peer support certification to the arc, and where it sits. · **Governs:** the peer support work in `arc/stage-1-access-and-frame.md`, the Peer Support Canada rows in `facts/requirements-by-gate.md`, and the Make Space line in `arc/throughlines.md`. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Gates](https://app.notion.com/p/d4969021f8da4529a41eafec0c323ca4) · [Tasks](https://app.notion.com/p/1dca49d1884242e28a2f2d21f1216be9)

## Decision

Make Space becomes a peer support programme run by a nationally certified peer supporter, and later a certified peer support mentor. The route: self-study now, a peer-led core training as the crisis-line hours finish, provisional then full Peer Support Canada certification during Leeds, and mentor certification when Make Space is handed to trained facilitators.

## What it makes possible

- **A helping record with no gap.** Crisis-line training starts Jan 2027 and the 250 service hours finish around Aug 2028. Peer work picks up in the same weekly slot from Sept 2028 and runs to the application. That is about four years of continuous, supervised helping experience by 1 Dec 2030, above even the three-year reading of UBC's requirement (verification register, item 1).
- **A stronger counselling file.** UBC's department page says it sets no minimum amount of relevant experience but weighs it in adjudication (ecps.educ.ubc.ca/?p=831, read 1 Nov 2026). A national certification against published Standards of Practice is legible evidence of that experience, with an external assessor.
- **Make Space with standing.** A certified practitioner, a code of conduct and a supervision record make the programme credible to funders, partner organisations and an ethics board, and make it ready to carry a measure once an ethics route exists (open fork 3).
- **The hand-over built in.** Mentor certification is what lets her train and supervise the facilitators who take Make Space on in Stage 3 (`throughlines.md` §3), and later run the Creative Health peer programme at Therapeutic Studio.

## Where it sits, and why there

- **Phase 1 (now → 2027): self-study only.** The free BC Peer Support Worker Training Curriculum read against the Make Space practice, plus the Peer Support Canada Standards of Practice and Code of Conduct. No cohort is added beside CPACC, crisis-line training and EPSE 482.
- **Phase 2, autumn 2028: core training.** A peer-led online cohort (PeerWorks Core Essentials, morning Pacific time) timed to start as the crisis service hours close. The four weekly crisis hours become the training hours, so the weekly load holds steady rather than growing.
- **Phase 2, 2029–2030: certification.** The practicum, then provisional certification, then 200 hours of formalized peer support and full certification before the application.
- **Stage 2: mentor certification,** alongside the MA, ready for the hand-over.

## What it does not replace

The prerequisites, research experience, the supervisor conversation and academic referees. Peer support is also not counselling: the application describes it as supervised peer work, and the rule in `throughlines.md` holds (the peer register and the clinical offer never share a page).
