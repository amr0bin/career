# 2026-11-05 · UBC Psychology Lecturer as the teaching benchmark, and a teaching dossier started now

> **Purpose:** the reasoning behind adopting UBC Psychology's teaching-stream Lecturer posting as the bar the teaching line is built to, and starting the teaching dossier and four anticipatory courses fifteen years ahead of the application. · **Governs:** `roles/3-adjunct-professor/README.md` (benchmark posting), `roles/3-adjunct-professor/teaching-dossier.md`, `roles/README.md` (ladder row 3), `arc/stage-3-the-question.md` (teaching record), `options/open-forks.md` fork 8, `facts/stability-and-figures.md` (Optionality row), `facts/verification-register.md` items 55–60. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Decisions](https://app.notion.com/p/3cdae687f6934e57a7e1d3510e2cf898)

## Decision 1 · Build the teaching line to the teaching-stream Lecturer bar, not only adjunct

- **What:** UBC Department of Psychology, **Lecturer** (Faculty of Arts, start 1 July 2027, deadline 5 Jan 2027; read 5 Nov 2026) becomes the benchmark posting for Role 3. Full-time, 24 credits a year, initial three-year term, renewable; $7,500–$8,000 a month (about $90,000–$96,000 a year) with UBC benefits. Requires a PhD in psychology or a related discipline at hire; prefers broad knowledge plus depth in a subdiscipline the department holds, course development and delivery, varied teaching techniques, and willingness to teach intro psychology, **introductory quantitative methods** and upper-level courses.
- **Why:** it is a bigger seat than the adjunct role already in the arc. Teaching stream at UBC continues past Lecturer into the Professor of Teaching ranks (**verify** item 55), which carry educational leadership and, at the top, tenure. A decade of learning design is exactly what most new PhDs lack, and the arc already builds the equity record, a disability research line and a counselling psychology doctorate (a "related discipline", **verify** item 56). It also meets the stability criterion: salary, benefits and pension on renewable terms.
- **Earliest clean match:** the final PhD year (~Jan 2040 application for a July start), with the defence scheduled before the start date.

## Decision 2 · Start the teaching dossier now, from past workshops

- **What:** a teaching dossier opens now. It records, factually and without featuring it, the one university course she has initiated, designed and taught (Learning for Success, University Canada West, 2020, classes of 400 for credit); she is not proud of the institution, so its visibility is her choice. The story is carried by the workshops already delivered that align with the goal roles: **Pronouns 101 / SOGI** (one core design adapted for several organisations) and **Intro to Psychological Health and Safety**. Each gets an entry: design, adaptations, evidence, reflection, and the roles it supports. Plan and design live in `roles/3-adjunct-professor/teaching-dossier.md`; evaluations, testimonials and anything with a person in it live in the private dossier store in Notion (house rules 2, 6).
- **Why:** the posting asks for syllabi, evaluations, peer reviews and evidence-based assessment. The SOGI adaptations are direct evidence of "a variety of tools and techniques"; the psychological health and safety workshop is the closest thing to teaching psychology content already done. Testimonials are easiest to get while hosts still remember the session.

## Decision 3 · Design four anticipatory courses as clearly labelled exemplars

- **What:** four course design exemplars, each grounded in study she has actually done, with subject matter resting on independently verifiable scholarship and her role stated as designer:
  1. **Ethics in Creative Practice** (BFA: cultural theory and non-western art history)
  2. **Learning, Engagement and Health** (neurobiology studies at Dalhousie)
  3. **Disability, Accommodation and Wellbeing: A Social Model Approach** (the Applied Project, then the MA thesis, then the dissertation; four versions)
  4. **Creative Health: Arts, Learning and Wellbeing** (the later capstone joining the three)
  Plus one course deliverable now: **Designing Accessible Learning**, a workshop series for instructors and TAs, which produces real evaluations.
- **Why:** together they form one teaching identity, *creative health, ethics and inclusion*, distinctive in a psychology department and continuous with Therapeutic Studio, art therapy and the research line. Course 3 is the strongest: research-informed teaching from a study she designed. A course with four dated versions is itself dossier evidence.

## Decision 4 · Treat quantitative methods as the highest-leverage early move

- **What:** the statistics prerequisite already planned (UBC EPSE 482, Jan–Apr 2027) is joined by self-study in R before Leeds, a methodologically strong Applied Project, advanced statistics in the MA, and TA'ing a methods course in the PhD with the aim of teaching it.
- **Why:** intro quantitative methods is named in the posting and is among the hardest courses for departments to staff. The same work strengthens the Applied Project, the MA thesis and the PhD application, so it costs nothing extra on the arc.
