# 2026-11-01 · The thesis line: social model, and the Applied Project as pilot

> **Purpose:** the reasoning behind two choices that fix the research line from Leeds to the PhD. · **Governs:** `arc/the-thesis-line.md` and every file that describes the Applied Project or the thesis question. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Decisions](https://app.notion.com/p/3cdae687f6934e57a7e1d3510e2cf898)

## Decision 1 · The Leeds Applied Project is the empirical pilot of the MA thesis

- **Why:** it is the one piece of MSc work that comes with ethics approval and a supervisor. Used as a pilot, it gives the 1 Dec 2030 application a tested design, real data and a supervisor who has seen the work, and it makes the CGS-M proposal concrete.
- **What it replaces:** the earlier readings, that the Applied Project *is* the corpus study, or that it is a separate interview study about learning design. The corpus study is finished alongside it and credited within it.

## Decision 2 · The thesis question names no diagnostic category

- **Why:** the social model locates disability in barriers, not in impairments. The question studies the accommodation process as a potential disabling barrier: what it asks students to prove, how long it takes, how often it makes them re-prove, and what it assumes about stability. Fluctuation is studied as an assumption built into the process, not as a type of student.
- **Anchor:** psycho-emotional disablism (Thomas; Reeve), from the Leeds tradition.
- **The question:** *Does the university accommodation process act as a disabling barrier in its own right, and through which of its design features does it shape disabled students' wellbeing and help-seeking?*
- **Consequence:** recruitment is by experience of the process, not by diagnosis. Wellbeing and help-seeking are read as effects of the barrier.
