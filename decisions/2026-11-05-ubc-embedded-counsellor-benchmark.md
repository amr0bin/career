# 2026-11-05 · UBC embedded counsellor postings as the benchmark for Role 2

> **Purpose:** the reasoning behind adopting two UBC Level B embedded counsellor postings as the bar the counselling line is built to. · **Governs:** `roles/2-campus-counsellor/README.md` (benchmark postings), `arc/stage-2-the-room.md` (MA electives), `facts/stability-and-figures.md`, `facts/verification-register.md` items 49–54. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Decisions](https://app.notion.com/p/3cdae687f6934e57a7e1d3510e2cf898)

## Decision 1 · Build Role 2 to the embedded counsellor bar

- **What:** UBC's Embedded Counsellor, Faculty of Science (JR26370) and Counsellor, Faculty of Education (JR26534), both AAPS Level B at $8,087.08–$12,117.08 a month, become the benchmark postings for the campus counsellor role. Every preferred qualification is mapped to a gate.
- **Why:** the embedded model joins counselling to a faculty's systems (consultation, staff training, programming, stepped-care triage, peer-programme supervision). It is the joined intake at faculty scale, and half its duties are her existing profession.

## Decision 2 · Treat the five-year clock and supervision as tasks, not a reason to wait

- **What:** the minimums include five years of clinical counselling and experience supervising master's practicum students, a post-master's clock that makes ~2039 the earliest clean match on the current arc. Clinical work stays continuous from conferral through the PhD and internship, hours are logged yearly, and supervision is pulled forward through three routes (PhD supervision training, co-supervision at the campus centre, CCPA CCC-S).
- **Why:** arriving at ~2039 with a doctorate, five clinical years and supervision experience puts her at the top of the range, and the same years are already in the arc.

## Decision 3 · Start the preferred qualifications before the MA, as learning

- **What:** in Phases 1–2: Stepped Care 2.0, single-session thinking, San'yas, 2SLGBTQIA+-affirming practice, student development theory, CACUSS, MHFA, SVPRO disclosure training, online addiction care training, and one co-designed staff training joining access and wellbeing. In the MA: career development and programme evaluation join group and family therapy in the elective plan.
- **Why:** each is open before the MA (to verify), none is practice (house rule 1), and each turns a line of the posting into evidence years ahead of the application.
