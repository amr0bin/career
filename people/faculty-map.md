## How this is used

Faculty fit is built by being in someone's field for years (reading, citing, attending, sending the preprint), not discovered in a final autumn. Twice a year (April and October), re-read two profiles, check "accepting students" status, and read one recent paper. Annually, revise the shortlist to three per institution. Interests verified 20 Sep 2026 (therapeutic-practice) and 6 Oct 2026 (10-stability); publications not yet read.

**The finding underneath the list:** no one at UBC or SFU publishes on the accommodation process and depression. That supports the neglectedness claim, and it means the supervisor will be someone adjacent who wants the question.
