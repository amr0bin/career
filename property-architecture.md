# Property Architecture

**Three web properties, split by audience rather than by topic**

Status: working note · August 2026
Companion to: `career.html`

---

## The principle

The three properties were being organised by subject matter, which does not hold — topics bleed into one another and every piece of work touches disability, learning, and mental health at once. Splitting by **who the work is for** holds, because audiences do not bleed. A hiring committee, a procurement officer, and a workshop participant are looking for different things, arrive by different routes, and are persuaded by different evidence.

| Property | Audience | What it holds | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **andrearobin.com** | The person | Credentials, publications, speaking, registration, bio | Being found and assessed as an individual |
| **Accessible Learning Labs** | Institutions | Consulting, case studies, the corpus study, review protocol, reframing bank | Being retained and cited by organisations |
| **Therapeutic Studio** | People | Make Space, Other Bodies, Slow Leaf, the creative health method | Being joined, and later taught to practitioners |

---

## andrearobin.com — the credibility space

The page a journal editor, a hiring committee, a doctoral supervisor, or a healthcare company lands on. It answers one question: who is this person and what standing do they have.

Holds the degrees, the registration when it exists, the peer-reviewed publications, the speaking record, the press, and a single clear statement of the specialism. It is deliberately thin — everything on it is verifiable.

**Move required:** the three consulting case studies currently live here and belong to Accessible Learning Labs. Left in place, the site does two jobs badly.

**Why it carries the personal name:** registration attaches to a person, not to a brand. Clients searching for a psychologist search a name, and a college's advertising standards apply to the individual registrant. This is also the property the clinical practice sits under, for the same reason.

---

## Accessible Learning Labs — the institutional space

The property organisations transact with. Its audience has budgets, procurement processes, and a problem they can name.

Holds the consulting offer priced as a specialism rather than a service, the three case studies rewritten in implementation and access language, the four written accessibility resources, the essential-requirements corpus study, the review protocol, the reframing bank, and the defensibility file schema.

**The positioning claim:** the scarcity is the intersection — disability studies, accommodation law, essential requirements, measurement, procurement, clinical placement access, and learning design held together — not any one component. Learning designers are numerous; this combination is not.

**Where the growth is:** the four written-but-unpublished resources are the cheapest available move. They convert directly into search presence, citation, and pricing power, and they cost an editing pass rather than new work.

---

## Therapeutic Studio — the practice space

The property people join rather than hire. Its register is peer-to-peer, and that register is the method, not the marketing.

Holds Make Space, the Other Bodies tools, Slow Leaf, and the year-long creative health curriculum. Later it holds the manualised version and the training system that teaches the method to other facilitators.

**The boundary that must hold:** Therapeutic Studio is the creative health *method*. It is not the clinical practice. A registered psychologist's private practice is a regulated service with intake, consent, records, and college advertising standards attached; the studio's invitation is explicitly not that. Delivering both from the same page collapses the peer-to-peer ethic, because it cannot survive being offered by someone holding a protected title in the same breath. The clinical caseload sits under the personal name; the method stays here.

---

## How the three feed each other

- The **method** developed in Therapeutic Studio becomes the subject of the research published under the **personal name**.
- That research is the evidence base **Accessible Learning Labs** sells against.
- Institutional work generates the case material the research draws on.
- The personal name carries the standing that makes both of the others credible.

None of the three is load-bearing alone. The circuit is.

---

## Open risk

Three properties is a substantial maintenance load for one person, and there will be a stretch during the doctoral years with perhaps four hours a week available for all of it. The architecture should be tested against that stretch rather than against a good month.

The likely answer is that each property has a minimum viable state — for the personal site, a current CV and publication list and nothing more; for Accessible Learning Labs, a static offer page with the deposited research artefacts; for Therapeutic Studio, the running sessions and nothing new. Defining those minima before they are needed is what prevents an unplanned collapse into one site during the busiest year.

## Review

Revisit when any of the following changes: registration is granted, the corpus study is published, or the consulting practice takes on a named specialism it did not have before.
