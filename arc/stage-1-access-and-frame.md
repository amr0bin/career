# Stage 1 · Access & Frame

> **Purpose:** the detail of Phase 1 *Ground* and Phase 2 *Frame*: getting inside a university, putting the work on the record, and building a thesis-route counselling application · **Governs:** Stage 1 work, closing conditions and carried/set-down rules (target years: now → Aug 2031) · **Carried out in Notion:** [Gates](https://app.notion.com/p/d4969021f8da4529a41eafec0c323ca4) · [Tasks](https://app.notion.com/p/1dca49d1884242e28a2f2d21f1216be9)

## What this stage makes possible

By August 2031 she is an Accessibility Advisor inside UBC or SFU, deciding accommodations, with a CPACC, a Leeds MSc in Disability Studies, Rights and Inclusion, a finished and preregistered corpus study on essential requirements, a submitted paper, around four years of supervised crisis-line and advisor helping experience, and a counselling application written by someone the department has already met. The first role on this path is already a six-figure, pensioned career: the UBC Accessibility Advisor was posted in spring 2026 at about $99,700–$155,400, and SFU's Disability Access Advisor sits at Grade 11, $98,297–$117,276 (see `facts/stability-and-figures.md` §3). Security arrives in Stage 1, not Stage 3.

The one question already runs underneath it: *how does learning design in education and work decide who is included,* which narrows in Phase 2 into the thesis question: whether the accommodation process is a disabling barrier in its own right (see `the-thesis-line.md`).

## Phase 1 · Ground (now → 2027)

**The work**

- **CPACC.** Apply to IAAP by about 10 Nov 2026, sit by mid-December (first week of January as the fallback), held by 31 Mar 2027. It's a dated, external, legible marker at low cost. Twelve weeks, then stop.
- **Crisis Centre BC, Crisis Services Responder.** The January 2027 cohort: about 110 hours of training plus 32 supervised, then 250 service hours at four a week. It starts the helping-experience clock, gives the file its first external supervisor, and answers a question no group facilitation can: is sitting with one person in acute distress, in real time, work she wants?
- **Apply for UBC and SFU advisor roles now.** She already meets the posted minimums, and the MSc is an upgrade rather than an entry requirement. Apply to every operational and term role in both units, too: a term role is the way in, and continuing status is the target, because the waiver and most benefits attach to continuing appointments. Watch weekly from March; the 2026 UBC posting closed 7 May.
- **Employment-contract IP check.** Read three clauses before anything is published from inside a job: IP assignment, moral-rights waiver, and outside activities.
- **First papers.** The **four-literature framework paper** (CJDS first choice: under 6,000 words) is drafted over the winter and submitted by mid-2027. The **corpus study design** gets a sampling frame, coding manual and pilot plan. The resources go public two before the Leeds application and two after, starting with the essential-requirements piece, revised against the AFMC technical-standards reform first.
- **Make Space documentation.** A session note and a reflective note after every session. First, decide the ethics route: no participant measure, consent form or instrument is used until an ethics board and a supervisor exist. Once one does (a Leeds or partner REB), a validated depression screen at the start and end of each cycle turns the practice into a programme with a defensible claim.
- **Prerequisite inventory and first course.** Check earlier coursework against both UBC's and SFU's lists and equivalency tables. Then statistics (UBC EPSE 482, Jan–Apr 2027) and abnormal psychology (UBC PSYC 300, summer 2027).
- **Living as the advisor.** A weekly half-page determination for the casebook; the instruments read in full (UBC LR7, SFU GP 26, the *Accessible British Columbia Act*, *Meiorin*, *Moore*); and the rooms: CACUSS 2027 at UBC (6–9 June) and AHEAD 2027 (12–16 July).

**Complete when** the CPACC is held; crisis-line training is done and weekly shifts are under way; at least two resources are public with DOIs; the four-literature paper is submitted; the corpus study design is written; Make Space has a documentation practice and a decided ethics route; the contract check is done; advisor applications are in, and a role inside a post-secondary institution is held (the target) or the search is running on the posting cycle.

**Carried:** the job search, the writing (one substantial piece a month), Make Space weekly, the studio on its standing order. **Set down:** enrolment beyond one prerequisite at a time, and new consulting scope. Fieldwork moves to fortnightly until the CPACC is passed.

**Feeds forward:** the role (a funding instrument, a source of logged helping hours, a seat among future referees); a public record a panel can read; the helping-experience clock.

**Bottlenecks, as tasks**

| Prevention question | Promotion question | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Advisor roles are mostly filled from inside. Is an outside application wasted? | What would being the internal candidate make possible? | Apply to every operational and term role in both units; present or attend at CACUSS 2027 at UBC; three informational conversations by Feb 2027 |
| The crisis cohort may be full | What would four years of supervised helping experience do to the file? | Line up three professional references before the form opens (it can't be saved); Northern BC Crisis Centre and Crisis Text Line are the parallel routes |
| The plan is about 500 hours against about 340 Tuesdays | What gets finished if three things are *sequenced* rather than cut? | Corpus study into Leeds year 1; Fieldwork fortnightly; two resources before the application, two after |

**Support that multiplies:** one mentor inside a BC accessibility unit (a quarterly check-in after the conversations); a co-presenter for CACUSS or AHEAD; the **nominators file**, started with five named people who get the record twice a year; 80,000 Hours one-to-one advising to pressure-test the theory of change; a fee-only planner for the two blank numbers (the annual cost of the life wanted, and the size of the MA-year buffer).

**Fallback, once:** if neither UBC nor SFU hires in this phase, the same role in the wider BC post-secondary sector, or an Accessible BC Act committee seat, keeps her inside the field while the search runs.

## Phase 2 · Frame (2027 → Aug 2031)

**The work**

- **Leeds MSc Disability Studies, Rights and Inclusion**, online and part-time, starting **September 2027**. The application is targeted for 31 Mar 2027 and the intake decided on 5 Jan 2027. Nine 15-credit modules, then the 45-credit **Applied Project, which is the empirical pilot of the MA thesis**, with the corpus study finished alongside it (see `the-thesis-line.md`). Target: project submitted Aug 2029. Every module essay becomes a public piece within a month. Fees are £12,750 in total, payable by module (£1,170 per module, £2,220 for the project); neither university's waiver covers Leeds.
- **The prerequisite ladder, one course a term:** statistics, abnormal psychology, cognitive psychology (PSYC 309, 2028), counselling theories (SFU EDUC 323, 2028), developmental psychology (SFU EDUC 322/464), and helping skills (SFU EDUC 423) **last, in summer 2030, so the skills are recent at application**. Every course is graded by **31 Dec 2030**. When a course and a Leeds module collide, the course wins: anything with a fixed external date outranks anything without one.
- **Helping experience.** 250 crisis-line service hours (target Aug 2028) and the reference letter, plus advisor-role hours logged with reflection from the first day. UBC's department page says two years or 450 hours, and its Calendar says three years; planning to three years keeps the December 2030 application safe.
- **Research experience.** Two-plus years as a volunteer or paid RA in a UBC or SFU lab whose work touches disability or depression; the corpus-study paper submitted (target Oct 2029); the thesis question shaped to one lab's programme in a one-page note; a **supervisor conversation** recorded in the decision log.
- **Global mental health, scoped.** LSHTM GHM202, with its policy report on depression and disability; an LSHTM PgCert from Oct 2029 is optional.
- **The gate year (Sept 2029 → Dec 2030):** the GRE if UBC confirms it still applies; three referees confirmed six months ahead (lab academic, Leeds supervisor, crisis-line or advisor supervisor); statements in her own words; both applications and the CGRS master's award in on **1 Dec 2030**.

**Complete when** the Leeds applied project is submitted; all prerequisites are graded by 31 Dec 2030; the 250 service hours and reference are in hand; research experience is on the record with a paper submitted; a prospective supervisor has agreed to a conversation; three referees have said yes; UBC and SFU applications are in.

**Carried:** the advisor role at full time (the only window where a degree and a full salary can be assumed to coexist), the crisis shift, the hours log, Make Space weekly with its note, writing at its monthly floor, the studio's kept Sunday. **Set down:** new consulting scope (existing clients held; one retainer secured before Stage 2); any reduction in earnings the programme doesn't require.

**Feeds forward:** a thesis-route file that reads as research-literate, a finished study the MA thesis extends, referees who have watched the work, and a buffer built while income is at its peak.

**Bottlenecks, as tasks**

| Prevention question | Promotion question | Task |
|---|---|---|
| SFU admits about 12 from 250–300. Is the master's a long shot? | What would the file look like if a supervisor were expecting it? | Lab apprenticeship, the preregistered study, three faculty conversations before 2030 |
| Studying her own employer's accommodation process raises a dual-role question | What data design makes the thesis stronger *and* clean? | Settle with the prospective supervisor before applying: another institution, a students' association, a supervisor's dataset, or national survey items |
| Leeds and the crisis shifts compete for the same weeks | What does the programme's four-year limit allow? | Take fewer modules in the service year; breaks between modules are part of the design |

**Support that multiplies:** the **staff tuition waiver**. UBC covers 12 undergraduate credits a year after three months' service, and SFU's AD 9-10 covers its own credit courses, so the role pays for the ladder. Staff development funds offset part of Leeds (SFU: $1,100 a year plus up to $1,000 for external courses). Then the Leeds supervisor (a future referee and co-author), a partner organisation for the applied project, the lab PI, Leeds School scholarships and Snowdon grants, and a peer cohort making the same move.

**Fallback, once:** if both programmes decline in the 2030 cycle, reapply in December 2031 with their feedback. A second cycle is normal, and the advisor role carries the year.
