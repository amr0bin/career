# The global line

> **Purpose:** where global mental health sits in the arc, at what level, and why. · **Governs:** global mental health study, research and roles from now to Optionality. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Gates](https://app.notion.com/p/d4969021f8da4529a41eafec0c323ca4) (view: *Global line*), [Opportunities](https://app.notion.com/p/44cece5e2b7c43bdb07ca4a0dab93637), [Decisions](https://app.notion.com/p/3cdae687f6934e57a7e1d3510e2cf898)

## The ceiling

The redesign proven on one campus is adapted for mental health systems where access gaps are largest. That means disability-inclusive, task-shifted, community-based care, built with disabled people's organisations in those settings, and it leads to advisory roles that shape how global mental health includes disabled people. This is the most neglected corner of the work, so it is where a contribution is worth the most.

## The recommendation: the postdoc is the main global stage

**Global mental health enters at research level, as a funded postdoc after the PhD (about 2040–42), with light rungs before it that keep the thread alive without crowding the core work.**

Why after the PhD rather than between the MA and the PhD:

- **There is something worth carrying.** By 2040 the dissertation has tested a redesign. A postdoc adapts a proven model. A credential taken earlier would study the field without anything of my own to bring to it.
- **The level matches.** Between the MA and the PhD, the counsellor role, art therapy and the PhD application already fill the time. Adding a global mental health degree there would compete with all three. After the PhD, global mental health becomes the main work.
- **Registration doesn't block it.** BC registration needs the doctorate, a 12-month pre-doctoral internship and three exams, with no postdoctoral hours required; a provisional licence covers up to 12 months while exams finish ([CHCPBC](https://chcpbc.org/applicants/psychology)). Sitting the EPPP and jurisprudence exams during the internship year lets registration and the postdoc run side by side.
- **Fellowships favour it.** Canadian postdoctoral fellowships can fund this, and some can be held abroad. A dissertation with a global aim makes the application specific.

## The rungs, at the right level for each stage

| Stage | Rung | Level | Cost to the core work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame, Leeds Year 2 (2028–29) | **Disability and Global Challenges** module, with the essay written as the global framing of the thesis question | Taught module, already in the programme | None |
| Frame (2027, optional) | LSHTM GHM202 single module | Taught module | Medium: it overlaps Leeds Year 1, so the default is to decline unless a funder or the question asks for it |
| Practice (~2035) | A short intensive, e.g. the [McGill Summer Program in Social and Cultural Psychiatry](https://www.mcgill.ca/tcpsych/training/summer) global mental health workshop | Professional development and contacts | Low: one to two weeks |
| The Question (2035–40) | A global or comparative **aim in the dissertation**; a global mental health researcher on the committee | Doctoral research | Low: it strengthens the dissertation |
| **Global (2040–42)** | **Funded postdoc in global mental health**: one partnered study adapting the redesign, with a disabled people's organisation, in a lower-resource or community setting | Independent research | This is the work |
| Optionality (2042 →) | Advisory roles; international partnerships for the centre | Leadership | n/a |

**Candidate hosts:** the Centre for Global Mental Health (LSHTM and King's College London); McGill's social and transcultural psychiatry division; CAMH; or a Canadian host with fieldwork abroad. **Funding:** Canadian tri-council and Banting fellowships (apply in autumn 2039, during the internship year), or host awards. All of these are marked *verify* until the 2030s.

## Fallback, once

If a funded global postdoc doesn't land in 2040, the global aim continues from Canada as a collaborative study run from the counsellor or faculty role, and the fellowship is tried again the following year.
