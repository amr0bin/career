# Stage 2 · The Room

> **Purpose:** the detail of Phase 3 *The Room* (the thesis MA, practicum and registration) and Phase 4 *Practice* (the counsellor role, Therapeutic Studio and the VATI diploma) · **Governs:** Stage 2 work, closing conditions and carried/set-down rules (target years: Sept 2031 → ~2035) · **Carried out in Notion:** [Gates](https://app.notion.com/p/d4969021f8da4529a41eafec0c323ca4) · [Calendar & Deadlines](https://app.notion.com/p/1206e45a346348b3a851aaed548b1cc5)

## What this stage makes possible

By about 2035 she is a registered clinical counsellor working inside a campus counselling service at UBC or SFU, specialising in depression and episodic and fluctuating conditions. She has an empirical thesis on the accommodation process as a disabling barrier, a practice open at Therapeutic Studio, art therapy training under way or complete, and a funded offer from UBC's PhD in Counselling Psychology. The counselling role isn't a detour from the accessibility work. SFU's service already publishes a pathway for disabled and neurodivergent students, and UBC's Wellness Advisor posting names "determining appropriate care levels within a stepped care model" as the core duty. **The governing question is the job description.**

Two things make this stage reachable. First, **the training pipeline is the hiring pipeline**: campus services train the people they later hire, so the practicum is a years-long interview conducted from the inside. Second, the employer is also the school.

## Phase 3 · The Room (Sept 2031 → ~2034)

**The work**

- **The thesis-based MA in Counselling Psychology** at UBC (48 credits plus a 6-credit thesis, CACEP-accredited) or SFU (52 units with an 18-unit thesis, evening classes, minimum eight terms, so a Sept 2031 start finishes around spring 2034). At SFU the MA is a **gate passed after entry**: faculty approval must be secured in Year 1, or the default is the MEd. An MEd closes the UBC PhD, which admits only from a thesis-based master's with an 80% average.
- **The thesis is the question.** *Does the university accommodation process act as a disabling barrier in its own right, and through which of its design features does it shape disabled students' wellbeing and help-seeking?* (social model; see `the-thesis-line.md`) That means documentation burden, wait times, denials and unmet need, measured against depressive symptoms and use of campus mental health services. It's designed from the first term, extends the Leeds corpus study, and the proposal is defended by the end of Year 2 (target Aug 2033). Data access and the dual role are settled with the supervisor before the proposal.
- **Funding in the first term.** The Canada Graduate Research Scholarship, master's ($27,000) is applied for with the application itself on 1 Dec 2030, so the proposal is written once. Then the BC Graduate Scholarship, departmental awards, a **Mitacs Accelerate** partnership ($15,000 per internship; non-profits qualify) with a disability organisation, and an RA post near the thesis, the most reliable route to a co-authored paper. At SFU, the CERi Graduate Fellowship. Research framing decides the funder: accommodation policy and students' experience fits SSHRC, and treating depression fits CIHR. Decide with the supervisor.
- **The practicum seat (the hinge).** UBC Counselling Services takes about four master's practicum students a year. Applications close **15 Jan (target 2033)** and need a 20–30 minute mock-session recording, two references and an interview: 600 hours, at least 180 direct, 2.5–3 days a week, mid-August to April. SFU takes students from any institution (streams close 30 Nov and 25 Jan), and SFU students secure their own placements in Year 2. This is the narrowest point in the whole arc.
- **Registration built to the higher bar.** RCC (BCACC) needs six core areas, including **group therapy and family therapy**, the two most often missing from transcripts, so they go into the elective plan in Year 1, alongside **career development and programme evaluation**, which UBC's embedded counsellor postings list (`../roles/2-campus-counsellor/README.md`, benchmark postings). It also needs 100 hours of clinical supervision with 25 direct. CCC (CCPA) needs 150 direct client hours. Plan to both. Apply the week of conferral, and track CHCPBC's psychotherapy standards (regulated from 29 Nov 2027), because no designation will be carried over automatically.

**Complete when** the thesis is defended; the degree is conferred; practicum hours are complete with group and family therapy on the transcript; the RCC application is submitted (and CCC eligibility documented); the practicum year's salary arrangement either held or was replaced as planned, and the reason is written down.

**Carried:** clinical training first; the advisor role at a negotiated fraction; Make Space at its floor (one session weekly); writing at one piece monthly; the studio's kept Sunday and the catalogue. In the practicum year the floor is the hours log alone. **Set down:** the full-time role in its current form, new consulting scope, and anything that competes with the placement days.

**Feeds forward:** registration; a defended thesis and a supervisor who has seen the research; practicum supervisors who become clinical referees and, later, colleagues; the first tri-council money on the record.

**Bottlenecks, as tasks**

| Prevention question | Promotion question | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Can a salaried role survive 2.5–3 days a week of placement? | What does a reduced appointment negotiated *at hire* make possible? | Raise reduced hours, study leave and secondment at the Stage 1 offer; plan the practicum-year income by 2032; SFU's agreement lists development leave of up to 12 months after six years (**verify**) |
| Four seats at UBC Counselling Services | What would it take to be the obvious pick for one? | Known to the service from Year 1; mock-session practice from the start; a self-secured SFU seat in parallel |
| SFU's thesis route isn't guaranteed at entry | What secures it early? | A named supervisor and a Year 1 approval plan written into the letter of intent |

**Support that multiplies:** the **staff tuition waiver** (SFU waives its own graduate programmes for continuing employees; UBC's covers up to $5,738.52 a year of a master's against first-year tuition of $8,204; SFU's MA is about $17,500 in total); the thesis supervisor and committee; practicum supervisors; Mitacs and the partner organisation; the employer's flexible arrangement; and, as a last resort, the RRSP Lifelong Learning Plan and student aid.

**Fallback, once:** if the practicum year can't fit the role, the online alternates still produce the RCC, at the cost of the UBC PhD route, and are weighed with that in view.

## Phase 4 · Practice (~2034 → 2035)

**The work**

- **Counsellor role at UBC or SFU** (UBC counsellor roles have been posted at about $91,000–$143,000; UBC Wellness Advisor at $7,283.75–$10,474.08 a month, AAPS). The specialism, depression and episodic and fluctuating conditions in disabled students, is a staffed pathway that's short of people. Emily Carr's counselling roles are reachable on registration too, with accessibility and counselling under one roof (**verify** whether permanent or contract).
- **Therapeutic Studio opens at RCC scope.** Small and deliberate, with a sliding scale from the first client. Insurance, records, advertising compliance and a dual-relationship policy are in place first. Make Space and Creative Health stay visibly separate as the peer programme. The naming question under the protected title is settled before the first client. Fees: the BCACC 2026 guide recommends $155–$175 for 50 minutes for experienced RCCs and $175–$225 for specialised services.
- **VATI Advanced Diploma in Art Therapy** (post-master's, about 15 months, distance stream with residential intensives in Vancouver). Apply **30 Mar** for a July start: target 30 Mar 2034 if VATI admits in the MA's final term (**verify**), otherwise July 2035. The placement is requested in post-secondary or community mental health with a disability population, and the site that trains her becomes the first referral partner. RCAT registration follows completion.
- **The PhD application** (autumn 2034 for Sept 2035): a supervisor confirmed; funding in writing; the tri-council doctoral application (CGRS D) in; a nomination for the university's flagship awards requested; a second publication on the record.

**Complete when** registration is held; the counsellor role is held; the first clients have been seen at Therapeutic Studio; VATI is held or in its final placement; the PhD offer is funded and accepted.

**Carried:** the counsellor role; the practice, small; the diploma; Make Space at its floor; the studio, now at full, because VATI's creative project is studio work. **Set down:** new consulting scope. The retainer carries the placement months.

**Feeds forward:** clinical data and standing in the dissertation's own setting; internal candidacy for the CPA-accredited internship at a campus centre; a second registration; RCC practice income that runs through the doctorate.

**Bottlenecks, as tasks**

| Prevention question | Promotion question | Task |
|---|---|---|
| VATI's tail overlaps PhD Year 1 | Which shared work lets both run? | Design VATI's creative project and placement around the dissertation population; if the overlap won't hold, enter the PhD in Sept 2036. The phase closes on conditions |
| RCC processing or the 2027 regulation moves the goalposts | What keeps practice open either way? | Practise under CCC while the RCC processes; match coursework to whichever body governs |

**Support that multiplies:** a clinical supervisor for the early practice; the VATI placement site as a referral partner; the doctoral supervisor, already in conversation; the nominators file, now about eight years deep.

**Fallback, once:** if the PhD isn't funded in the first cycle, reapply with another publication. MA + RCC + a campus-centre role already deliver practice, sessional teaching and supervision.
