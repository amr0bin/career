# Stage 3 · The Question

> **Purpose:** the detail of Phase 5 *The Question* (UBC PhD Counselling Psychology, internship, psychologist registration) and Phase 6 *Optionality* (the seats held together, some of them built) · **Governs:** Stage 3 work, closing conditions and carried/set-down rules (target years: ~2035 →) · **Carried out in Notion:** [Gates](https://app.notion.com/p/d4969021f8da4529a41eafec0c323ca4)

## What this stage makes possible

Research independence and the protected title. In BC the psychologist title runs through one door. Registration requires a doctorate; UBC's PhD in Counselling Psychology is CPA-accredited and admits only from a thesis-based master's; SFU has no counselling psychology doctorate. A registered psychologist may diagnose, and UBC accepts learning-disability documentation only from a registered psychologist or certified school psychologist. Someone who holds that authority *and* knows accommodation law, essential requirements and disability studies stands on both sides of the gate that decides who gets access. That's the position from which the centre, the redesigned documentation standard, the research chair and the standards seats all become reachable.

It's also the leadership tier of the campus service. At UBC Counselling Services that tier is almost entirely PhD, EdD or R.Psych. The master's gets the room; the doctorate gets the service.

## Phase 5 · The Question (~2035 → 2040; registration ~2041)

**The work**

- **UBC PhD Counselling Psychology**, entered funded, with a supervisor who wants the question. 51 credits, specialty comprehensive exam, dissertation, and a **1,600-hour CPA-accredited pre-doctoral internship**, served where possible at UBC Counselling Services, whose internship is CPA-accredited, so the credential and internal candidacy come together.
- **The dissertation moves from levers to intervention.** The MA thesis found which parts of the accommodation process drive depression. The dissertation tests a response: a joined access-and-mental-health intake, or accessible low-intensity provision (Creative Health among the candidates), built to reach the students the thesis found left out. Research methods are chosen for the question: feasibility first, single-case design, qualitative depth (IPA, narrative inquiry), with a statistician and co-authors on the team.
- **Milestones, re-dated (targets):** comprehensive exam (a review manuscript submitted to a journal) by end of 2037; **dissertation proposal defended and candidacy within 36 months of entry (by Aug 2038)**, the one hard clock in the stage; internship matched through APPIC by 2039 (eligibility needs coursework complete, at least 600 practicum hours and an *approved* proposal before applying, so the proposal comes early); dissertation defended ~2040; CHCPBC psychologist registration applied for (EPPP and oral exam), granted ~2041.
- **Funding as a condition of entry.** UBC minimum funding is $40,000 a year for four years. CGRS D is $40,000 a year for three. Killam is $40,000 a year for two. Trudeau is up to $50,000 a year for three plus research and travel (years one or two only). Add the UBC Four Year Fellowship and **UBC Public Scholars** (up to $10,000, renewable once) with Make Space, a disability organisation or an accessibility office as partner.
- **Teaching record.** TAships and the graduate teaching certificate, building the dossier an adjunct or Professor of Teaching appointment reads.
- **The PhD-only prerequisites**: psychology of oppression (partly covered by Leeds), career and vocational, social and cultural, biological bases, history of psychology, cognitive assessment, ethics, interventions. Mapped to MA electives and the Leeds modules before entry.

**Complete when** the dissertation is defended, the CPA-accredited internship is complete, and psychologist registration has been applied for.

**Carried:** the dissertation; the practice held small (enough to keep RCC, and RCAT if held, current); the counsellor role at whatever fraction the programme permits. **Set down:** everything expandable. Consulting is hard-capped at a named monthly ceiling set at entry. Make Space is **handed to a trained facilitator**, which is also the first test of whether the method transfers.

**Feeds forward:** the protected title, diagnostic authority, a research programme with its own funding, and a supervisor network that becomes collaborators.

**Bottlenecks, as tasks**

| Prevention question | Promotion question | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Is psychologist registration around 2041 too far out? | What is already secure in 2031, 2034 and 2037? | Each stage is a complete career before the next: advisor, then RCC counsellor, then a funded doctoral researcher |
| The internship match can fail | What would make a campus centre expect her? | Build the relationship through the MA practicum years earlier; second-phase match, 24-month half-time and approved non-APPIC sites are named alternatives |
| Can the PhD be taken part-time after candidacy? | What arrangement keeps the practice and the role alive through it? | **Verify** with UBC before applying; settle the salary fraction in writing before entry |

**Support that multiplies:** the supervisor and committee; the department that nominates for Killam and Trudeau; research collaborators, a statistician and co-authors; Public Scholars partners; the nominators file, now about a decade deep; and the RCC practice, which lets income continue through the degree. As illustration only, `10-stability` sums six client hours a week for 44 weeks at $165 to about $43,600, beside $40,000 of funding.

**Fallback, once:** MA + RCC + a campus-centre role already yields practice, sessional teaching and adjunct standing. The PhD widens the ceiling; it doesn't hold up the floor.

## Phase 5b · Global (postdoc, ~2040 → 2042)

A funded postdoc in global mental health. The redesign the dissertation tested is adapted, with a disabled people's organisation, for a lower-resource or community setting. Registration finishes alongside it: the EPPP and jurisprudence exams are sat during the internship year, and BC requires no postdoctoral hours. The level, the rungs before it and the candidate hosts are in `the-global-line.md`.

**Complete when** the partnered study is done and submitted with local co-authors, and registration is complete. **Feeds forward:** international collaborators, a fellowship record, and the global seat in Optionality.

## Phase 6 · Optionality (~2042 →)

**The work.** The fractional shape: several seats at once, none of them full-time, some applied for and the most important ones built. A fractional career already held by a BC scholar-practitioner (counselling master's and clinical registration, an interdisciplinary PhD, an adjunct appointment, and a primary career in university equity and academic leadership) is the proof that the pieces can be held together. It's the starting shape, not the ceiling.

- **Build:** a joined access-and-mental-health centre at UBC or SFU, proposed with a sponsor who carries it to a dean, funded and led. The master's thesis and the dissertation are its evidence base.
- **Redesign the gate:** an accessible, lower-cost assessment service (private psychoeducational assessments in the Lower Mainland run about $3,300–$3,900 each) and a published documentation standard.
- **Lead:** a director-level seat in accessibility, equity or counselling services.
- **Research:** tri-council operating grants as principal investigator, doctoral students, a Michael Smith Health Research BC trainee award early on ($60,000 a year plus allowance, up to three years, postdoctoral), Vancouver Foundation participatory grants with Creative Health as a partner, and in time a research chair nomination.
- **Standards:** Accessibility Standards Canada technical committees, the College's registrant committees, professional boards. One at a time.
- **Teach:** adjunct in a counselling department, clinical supervision of counselling and art therapy students.
- **Practise:** Therapeutic Studio at psychologist scope (individual clients plus the Creative Health peer programme, art therapy within it), sized by desire and priced by need.
- **Global mental health:** advisory, research and policy roles on how depression care scales, carrying the disability question into the field that sets the global agenda.

**Complete when** it doesn't close. Review twice a year: are the seats still held, and is any one becoming full-time at the others' expense? The test for any new commitment: *would I take this if I did not need the money?* Horizon: institutional work held to about 75, health permitting, then by choice the practice, the studio and some consultation.

**Carried:** all of it, at chosen volumes. **Set down:** anything that makes one seat full-time at the cost of the others.

**Support that multiplies:** sponsors inside the university, funders, and a team of clinicians, researchers and trained Creative Health facilitators who deliver the work when she isn't in the room. One asset (a licensed training system or course) earns without her presence.

**Fallback, once:** each seat stands alone. Losing any one is an inconvenience, not an emergency.
