# 15 · Across the Arc: Where This Practice Pays Off Elsewhere

## Overview

Learning design is not only the job you do now. Its methods are the same methods the research, clinical and teaching work ask for, in different language. Seeing the match saves you from learning each one twice, and it is a real comparative advantage: few counselling applicants or researchers arrive already fluent in needs assessment, alignment, piloting, QA and evaluation.

## Three Connections Worth Acting On Now

1. **Your design practice is research evidence.** The UBC counselling application weighs research literacy and relevant experience. A learning-design case study with a beta test, QA data and an evaluation report shows research-adjacent method. Write case study 1 with that reader in mind.
2. **The construct/method distinction is one idea used three times.** It drives assessment design (05), the corpus study's headline code and the accommodation memo. Use the same language in all three, so a panel sees one coherent expert rather than three jobs.
3. **Group facilitation is clinical preparation.** Workshop design and facilitation are close to psychoeducational group work, which the RCC requires as a core area. Keep a facilitation log now, separate from Make Space, and it becomes evidence for the MA file and the group-therapy elective.
