# 13 · Freelance Practice — Accessible Learning Labs

## Overview

The workflow in this folder was written for an institutional team. Freelance work through Accessible Learning Labs uses the same craft, plus four things an employer normally supplies: a clear offer, a contract, a price, and a boundary with the day job. This file covers those four, then shows which workflow stages each kind of engagement uses.

> **The two rules that govern every engagement** (from `../philosophy/`): *Open the resources; price the method* — open, CC-licensed resources are not unpaid consulting. And the consulting test: **would I take this if I didn't need the money?** Consulting expands to fill whatever room it is given.

## The Offer: Three Engagement Shapes

Keep the menu short. Each shape uses a subset of the 20 stages, so scoping is faster and quotes are consistent.

| Shape | What the client gets | Workflow stages used | Typical evidence of value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accessibility & UDL review** | A prioritised review of a course, programme, document set or process, with fixes and owners | 3, 6, 15, 18 (see also `../structures/ACCESSIBILITY_AUDIT_REPORT.md`) | Issues found and fixed; re-test results |
| **Course or module (re)design** | An aligned, accessible course or module built with the client's SME | 3–7, 9–16, 19 | Launch on scope; beta and QA results; sustainment plan in place |
| **Workshop or capacity building** | A facilitated session or short series that leaves the client's team able to do it themselves | 3, 6, 7, 12, 17 | Pre/post confidence, a team artefact, adoption of a template |

**Process review is the distinctive offer.** Most reviewers check artefacts (PDFs, videos, LMS pages). Your corpus study and thesis question make you unusually good at reviewing *processes*: accommodation request forms, documentation demands, deadlines, re-application cycles. Name it explicitly in the review offer.

## Discovery and the Statement of Work

Run the intake conversation from `02_project_intake_and_scoping.md`, then send a **Statement of Work (SOW)** before any work starts. Template: [`templates/statement-of-work.md`](templates/statement-of-work.md).

An SOW states:
- The problem in the client's words, and the outcome in yours
- Deliverables, each with an acceptance criterion
- What is out of scope (copy the MoSCoW "Won't" list)
- The client's responsibilities: SME hours, access, review turnaround
- Timeline and review cycles
- Fees, payment schedule and what triggers a change order
- Intellectual property and licensing (below)
- Accessibility of the deliverables themselves (they must meet the standard they recommend)

**Change orders:** any request outside the SOW gets a short written change order (what, cost, time) before work starts. This is the freelance form of `10_stakeholder_and_sme_collaboration.md` § Scope Creep.

## Pricing the Method

Price by **engagement shape and deliverable**, not by the hour, wherever scope is clear; quote hourly only for open-ended advisory time, with a cap.

1. Estimate effort per stage with the Overhead/Effort Estimate (`templates/effort-estimate.md`), including your own QA, admin and two review cycles.
2. Multiply by your day rate, then add a contingency (commonly 10–20%) for review delays.
3. Quote a fixed fee per deliverable, with payment tied to milestones (e.g. 40% on signing, 40% on beta, 20% on launch).
4. Record actual hours against the estimate after each project; adjust the next estimate from the data, not from memory.

Set the day rate from verified market data before quoting (association surveys, posted contract rates, peers). Rates and figures belong in `../facts/stability-and-figures.md` once verified, not here.

## IP, Licensing and Credit

- **Default:** the client owns the course they paid for; you keep the right to reuse your *methods, templates and frameworks* and to describe the work in a portfolio (anonymised if needed). Say so in the SOW.
- **Open resources** (Groundwork workbooks, Fieldwork, landscape repos) stay under **CC BY or CC BY-SA** and are never transferred. A client can be licensed to adapt them; they don't buy them.
- **Disabled contributors** (reviewers, co-designers, testers) are paid and credited by name, with their consent (house rule 7).
- **AI use** is disclosed to the client in the SOW and follows `09_tools_and_technology.md` § Ethical considerations.

## The Boundary with the Day Job

Read the employment contract before taking freelance work, and record the date you read it (`../arc/stage-1-access-and-frame.md`, *Employment-contract IP check*):
- **IP assignment:** does the employer own work made on your own time? Make freelance work on your own time and equipment, from public sources only.
- **Outside activities and conflict of interest:** declare freelance work if the policy requires it. Never take a client whose work your employer oversees or funds, and never use an employer artefact as a starting point (house rule 3).
- **Moral rights:** check whether you waived them; it affects how you can credit your own past work.

When you hold an advisor role, decline any engagement where you might later adjudicate the same institution's students or staff.

## Working with Collaborators

You plan to bring colleagues in during 2027. The SME onboarding kit in `10_stakeholder_and_sme_collaboration.md` doubles as a **collaborator onboarding kit**:
- A one-page "how we work" document: the workflow stages we use, quality bar, accessibility standard, review norms
- A subcontract with the same IP and credit terms as the client SOW
- The shared templates folder, so every deliverable looks and works the same
- A short QA pairing: each collaborator's first deliverable is reviewed against `templates/qa-checklist.md`

## Running the Business (BC)

- **Records:** one folder per client (SOW, change orders, deliverables, invoices, case-study notes) — never mixed with employer files.
- **Invoices:** number, date, deliverable, amount, payment terms, and GST if registered.
- **GST/HST:** registration is required once taxable revenue passes the **$30,000 small-supplier threshold** in four consecutive calendar quarters; you may register voluntarily earlier. Confirm the current rule with the CRA or an accountant.
- **Insurance:** consider professional liability insurance before larger contracts; some institutional clients require it.
- **Privacy:** if you handle learner data for a BC public institution, the client's FIPPA obligations apply to you through the contract; for private clients, PIPA.

Verify each of these with an accountant or the relevant authority before relying on it; add confirmed facts to `../facts/`.

## Closing Every Engagement

1. Hand over the sustainment plan (`templates/sustainment-plan.md`) so the client can maintain the work.
2. Ask for a short testimonial and permission to describe the project.
3. Write the case study (`templates/case-study.md`) within two weeks, while details are fresh.
4. Log hours against the estimate.
