# The Five Ledgers and the November Review

> **Purpose:** the measure that replaces feeling — five ledgers, one annual question, one review · **Governs:** how progress is judged across every stage and stream · **Carried out in Notion:** [Weekly Log](https://app.notion.com/p/37d529eede154f419bc813a96fa88430)

This file is the **design**. The entries themselves live in Notion; private records (service hours, supervision, references) live in private stores and never in this repo.

---

## Why ledgers

A year where nothing moved feels identical to a busy year. Without a record, the failure this system exists to catch is invisible. The ledgers make it visible, and they replace a feeling about a field with a list of dated entries.

**Accumulation, not intensity.** A year in which all five ledgers gained one small entry is a year on the trajectory. A heroic month that produced nothing recorded is not. This profession, in particular, punishes heroic months.

---

## The five

One set of ledgers for the whole arc. Each has an access side and a practice side; the studio keeps its own accounts on the same pattern.

### 1 · Corpus — what has been made or earned

- **Access:** the resource library — written resources, workbooks, protocols, the landscape. Versioned, deposited with DOIs, openly licensed.
- **Practice:** service hours, training, supervision, prerequisites passed, certificates; later practicum hours, modalities, registration. **Private and externally attested** — by a supervisor, an institution, and eventually a registrar. Confidentiality is the condition of clinical work; no amount of de-identification turns a person's material into a public record.
- **Same job, different mechanism:** verification by someone who is not you.
- **Question:** *did it gain an entry a reader — or a registrar — would accept?*

### 2 · Shown — the public record

- Talks, workshops, webinars, conference presentations, a committee seat; the notebook and the spoken piece; credited practitioner interviews; documented resources.
- **Absolute limit:** nothing client-derived, ever (`house-rules.md`).
- **Question:** *did something go out with a date on it?*

### 3 · Funded — money moving toward the work

- **Access:** paid consulting and workshops at the specialism's rate; later the salaried post.
- **Practice, pre-registration:** nothing can be charged for, so the ledger holds money moving *toward* the work — waiver-funded prerequisites, funded training, bursaries, stipends, travel support.
- **The rule:** *nothing free that should be paid*, and its pre-licence form, *nothing absorbed that should be funded*.
- **Question:** *did someone else pay for part of this?*

### 4 · Scholarly — the research record

- The conceptual paper, the corpus study, the scoping review stage by stage (protocol → screening → extraction → synthesis → preprint → journal → conference), Leeds assessments, the Applied Project, later the MA thesis and dissertation.
- The one ledger that can be closed without an institution's permission, and the one the MA explicitly adjudicates.
- **Question:** *did a piece move one stage?* One stage a year is behind; two is on pace.

### 5 · Relational — the people

- Disabled practitioners and organisations; advisors in the UBC and SFU units; Leeds staff; referees and the date each was asked; supervisors; named people inside the counselling services who decide practicum seats; the standing reader.
- **The only ledger that cannot be produced in the year it is needed**, and the one most applicants are thinnest in.
- Kept as roles and dates, not as a feeling about a field.
- **Annual rate:** two new named relationships, each kept warm with two contacts a year.
- **Question:** *did a named person who can open a door learn your name this year?*

## The yearly question

One paragraph a year, under two hundred words, dated, **appended below last year's and never overwritten**.

**What it answers:** what the year's work found out about the throughline question — *does the accommodation process itself drive depression in disabled students, and which parts of it are the levers?* Not what was done. Not what the work is about. What was learned: where the hypothesis held, where it failed, what it could not see.

**Why:** every application, supervisor conversation, reference request and doctoral file asks a version of this. Eight dated versions are evidence of sustained independent inquiry that no coursework can produce. Each statement of interest is written from this record rather than reconstructed under deadline.

**How:** in the register of the notebook, not application language. Written after the ledgers are reviewed, against a year of actual work. Answered from the literature, the room in general terms, and her own thinking — **never from anyone who came to her**. The first one is allowed to be uncertain; the distance between the first and the fifth is itself the evidence.

## The November review

**When:** once a year, on the first Wednesday of November. It sits a month before the 1 December application deadline in every year that has one, so the review is done while there is still time to act.

**How long:** ninety minutes.

**In order:**

1. **Close the logs.** Everything from the year recorded before anything is assessed.
2. **The five questions.** Write the answer, not a score — one line each — and the one thing to add next year.
3. **Did the floor hold in the worst month?** If not, was the floor wrong, or the month?
4. **Is the file closer to the next gate than a year ago?** Which row of the target posting or admission requirement moved?
5. **Reread the ceiling** (`ceiling-and-floor-in-practice.md`) and ask whether the ceilings and the hinge are still the right targets. They will change; the file is revised, not obeyed.
6. **Strike verified items, add new ones** in [Verify Queue](https://app.notion.com/p/243eb88582cf4222a2bb7ffc4abda33a).
7. **Write the yearly question** paragraph.
8. **Read the floor against next year's shape.** If a hard stretch is coming, say so now and drop to the floor deliberately. *Expect it and it is a season; discover it and it feels like failure.*

Then close the file until November.

**Reading the result.** All five moved: a good year, whatever it felt like. Three moved: still on the trajectory. None moved: the failure this system exists to make visible, now visible in time to act.

The first review is a baseline. Most ledgers will be thin, and that is the point: the second review has something to compare against.
