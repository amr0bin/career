# Ceiling and Floor in Practice

> **Purpose:** what the ceilings actually are, what the floor is, and why stability belongs inside the ambition · **Governs:** how each stage sets its targets and its minimums · **Carried out in Notion:** {{notion:Gates}}

The protocol (`ceiling-and-floor-protocol.md`) says how to talk about the future. This file says what the ceiling and the floor *are*, concretely, on this path.

---

## The ceiling, stated once

A clinician-researcher who holds the clinical hour, the accommodation file and the research programme at once, and who uses that position to build the service, the evidence and the standards the next generation of disabled students will meet. Founding and leading a university centre where access and mental health are one intake rather than two. Past that: redesigning the documentation gate itself, a national standards voice on essential requirements, a funded research programme, and an art practice held seriously throughout.

Every stage is built to make that person real early. A file assembled in the year it is needed reads as assembly. **A file that accumulated for eight years reads as a life.**

## Three levels for every gate: met, strong, excellent

Borrowed from Access Lab and applied to every gate on the arc:

- **Met** is what the gate requires. It is **the floor that never slips**.
- **Strong** is what a well-prepared candidate brings.
- **Excellent** is what makes a reader remember the file. **Excellent is the target.**

| Gate | Met | Excellent |
|---|---|---|
| CPACC | Passed | Passed, then *used*: community joined, credential placed where the postings' knowledge lines sit |
| Leeds | Entry route satisfied; modules passed | Admitted as a practitioner-researcher; every assessment public within a month; the Applied Project with a partner and disabled co-researchers |
| Advisor role | Shortlisted on the equivalence clause | Already known to the unit, already published on the problem the office finds hardest |

(*Strong*, between them, is the prepared candidate: passed first time, one question throughout, inside the unit at any level.)

## The two ceilings of Stage 2, and the hinge between them

The counselling stage has two ceilings in sequence, with a narrow point between.

**Ceiling one — the admissions panel, 1 December 2030** (**verified**: UBC and SFU both close 1 December for September entry). It reads prerequisites, supervised helping experience, research experience, fit with a faculty member's research, and references. The part straight-through applicants cannot fill is *standing*: years of supervised service, a published piece of research, an employer inside the institution, and a question already formed.

**The hinge — a practicum seat, ~January 2033.** The narrowest point in the whole sequence, and it is not the degree. UBC Counselling Services takes a small number of master's practicum students a year, with a recorded mock counselling session as part of the application (**verified**, 20 September 2026; recheck each cycle). Training inside the service is the documented route to being known by the people who later hire. Two things follow and are planned, not discovered: the practicum year is incompatible with full-time work, so income is a planning problem years ahead; and the mock-session recording is a real artefact with a real standard, rehearsed from now.

**Ceiling two — the hiring panel, ~2033–34.** A UBC Counselling Services wellness role's core duty is *determining appropriate care levels within a stepped care model*. SFU publishes a named counselling pathway for disabled and neurodivergent students. **The governing question is not merely compatible with the job; it is the job description.** Two honest qualifications: the master's is the floor in that service, not the differentiator; and the leadership tier is almost entirely doctoral. *The master's gets the room. The doctorate gets the service.*

## The floor

The floor is the minimum rate of accumulation that survives the hardest year. **It is decided now, in a season that is not hard, because a floor decided during a hard year is not a floor — it is whatever was left.**

Running at the floor is **success, not neglect**. A degree is not a pause in the practice; it is the practice at full intensity. Only one property runs at full intensity at a time.

### The floors that are never traded

Across every stage, including the worst one, three things have a minimum that is defended and a maximum that is refused:

- **One Make Space session weekly.**
- **One substantial piece of writing monthly.**
- **One kept studio Sunday weekly, and the catalogue entry for every sheet.** The catalogue never lapses, even when a Sunday does.

They are not what the plan can spare. They are the raw material every later stage draws on.

### Stream floors, by season

| Season | Floor |
|---|---|
| Any lost week | The record line written anyway: *no session, and why*. A gap that is recorded is a pause; a gap that is not is a disappearance |
| Leeds years (2027–29) | Coursework, one piece a year rewritten as a public resource, one talk, the relational list kept warm. On the practice side: one notebook entry a month, the service record updated within 24 hours, the crisis-line shift kept, one calendar check a month |
| The counselling MA (2031–33) | Access at its minimum viable state: the deposited resources findable and current. No new writing |
| The practicum year | The placement *is* the practice. The service record, and nothing else |

**The failure the floor is written against** is not dramatic: the workload rises, the weekly habit stops, the record stops with it because the habits were bundled, and a year later there is no way to tell when it stopped. **Unbundle the record from the rhythm**, so the record survives when the rhythm does not.

## Stability is not the opposite of ambition

It is the precondition for the largest version of the work. Three verified findings (10-stability-and-the-ceiling.md, 6 October 2026) change the picture:

1. **Security arrives at Stage 1, not Stage 6.** The first role on the path, an Accessibility Advisor post at UBC or SFU, is already a pensioned, six-figure career at the published ranges.
2. **Every later stage has funding that can be named**: staff tuition waivers, master's and doctoral scholarships, and a UBC doctoral funding minimum.
3. **In BC the psychologist title runs through one door**, UBC's PhD in Counselling Psychology, which admits only from a thesis-based master's.

Hence the working rule: **no stage that reduces income is entered before it has been sized and funded.** For the doctorate, funding is a condition of entry with the same standing as the supervisor. And the structure aims at optionality: several uncorrelated sources, none load-bearing, so no single stream can coerce a decision.

## The impact lens

Work is chosen by asking how much good it does, using the strengths already held. The lens ranks problems by **scale, neglectedness and solvability**, adds **personal fit**, and treats early stages as building career capital: skills, connections, credentials, character and runway.

**Scale:** about a third of first-year students in Canada report a disability (CUSC 2025), and depression is among the leading causes of disability worldwide (WHO). **Neglectedness:** no local researcher holds the throughline question. **Solvability:** the levers are policies universities control for every student at once. **Fit:** applied accessibility and learning design over the past decade, the Leeds degree, a role inside the system being studied. **Runway is career capital:** stability is part of the impact case, not a retreat from it. The lens is a method; it is re-run when the scoping review reports.

**Fallback, named once:** if a doctorate is never funded, the counselling registration, the practice, the university role and sessional teaching still deliver most of this life.
