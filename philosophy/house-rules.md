# House Rules

> **Purpose:** the boundaries that let the public side of the work stay public and the clinical side stay trustworthy · **Governs:** everything published, recorded or applied for, across all three stages · **Carried out in Notion:** [Tasks](https://app.notion.com/p/1dca49d1884242e28a2f2d21f1216be9)

These are stricter than the law currently requires, on purpose. If a draft blurs one of these lines, that is said before anything else — before the writing is discussed, before the idea is assessed. The line is the condition of the work, not a feature of it.

---

## 1 · The pre-licensure boundary

Psychotherapy becomes a regulated profession in BC on **29 November 2027** under the College of Health and Care Professionals of BC (**verified**, chcpbc.org/legislation/regulating-psychotherapy, checked 6 October 2026). Everything published before then should already read as if that date had passed.

### Words

- **Use:** facilitator, arts-based facilitator, creative health practitioner, peer.
- **Never:** counsellor, therapist, psychotherapist, clinician — or anything that implies one: *sessions*, *clients*, *treatment*, *intervention for depression* offered as a service.
- Make Space is a creative health practice and a public inquiry. **It is not therapy**, and its page says so once, plainly, without apology.
- "Therapeutic" in the studio's name describes the register of the work, not a service.

### Acts

- No one-to-one work framed as help with a mental health problem. Group, peer and open sessions only.
- No assessment, diagnosis or clinical advice — in the room, in comments, or by direct message. A standing reply names the provincial crisis line and 9-8-8.
- **No screening measures, outcome data, participant research or pre/post measures before the MA's ethics oversight.** Anything needing a consent sheet waits for a supervisor and a research ethics board.
- **Referral, not retention.** If someone needs more than the room, say so kindly and name where to go.

### Records

Two ledgers, never mixed. **Public:** the writing, the notebook, the tools, the resource library. **Private:** the reflective journal, incident notes, service and supervision records, references — in a separate private store, never in a public repository. Anything with a person in it is **anonymised at the point of writing, not later**.

### Respect for the licensed

Cite and credit registered practitioners and their frameworks by name. Interview registered practitioners for the notebook, credited. Read the BCACC Code of Ethics and the CATA standards now and hold the work to them voluntarily. When the College publishes its standards, reread every public page against them.

## 2 · Nothing client-derived, ever

Not in the public record, not in the yearly question, not in a paper, not de-identified. The question is answered from the literature, the room in general terms, and her own thinking — never from anyone who came to her. This holds at every stage, before and after registration.

## 3 · Never from your own workplace

Volunteering, peer programmes and lab work at the institution that employs you need the employer's written knowledge, and its outside-activities and conflict-of-interest policy read first. **Students who are or could be your advisees are not peers you support.** One employment contract governs three properties: read its IP assignment, moral-rights and outside-activities clauses once, and record the date read. No employer artifact is ever a starting point.

## 4 · No AI on the application itself

The system prepares the record, the training plan, the calendar and the evidence. **It does not draft the statement of interest, the reference requests in her voice, or the video answers.** Those are hers alone, as the department requires. No AI touches the application folder for any programme.

## 5 · Never use a real accommodation case to show adjudication skill

The gap between her record and the advisor posting is narrow: deciding, on an institution's behalf, whether a person is entitled to accommodation. It is closed with **invented cases, clearly marked as invented**, or not at all. No real case, ever, even de-identified. Nothing about students, clients or a real file goes public to evidence the skill. And no claim about students goes public before consent, privacy law and ownership of the evidence are settled; every piece is labelled as quality assurance or research under TCPS 2.

## 6 · Plans private, products public

Planning documents — targeting, odds, internal-candidate tactics, application strategy — are private. A panel member at UBC or SFU should never be reading her tactics about their office. What goes public is written as a **product**: resources, papers, the landscape evidence, analyses of public postings. Standpoint is hers to disclose where and when she chooses; a planning repository is not a place she chose deliberately.

This repository is the exception that proves the rule: it holds *why*, not tactics, and nothing personal.

## 7 · Openly licensed accessibility resources

Accessibility resources are licensed **CC BY or CC BY-SA**, deposited with version DOIs under one concept DOI, versioned and dated. Adoption by other institutions is the evidence of value, which is why the licence matters more here than anywhere else. Every repository carries a LICENSE file: **no licence means all rights reserved**, the opposite of the intention. Made on own time, own equipment, public sources, with a dated record of every draft.

**Open the resources; price the method.** Open work does not become unpaid consulting. Disabled contributors are credited by name and paid — *nothing about us without us* and *nothing free that should be paid* point the same way.

## 8 · "Over the past decade"

Experience is described as **over the past decade**, everywhere: CVs, sites, statements, repositories. Not "fifteen years". One phrase, used consistently, so no reader finds two numbers.
