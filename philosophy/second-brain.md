# The Second Brain

> **Purpose:** how knowledge flows from what I read and notice into what I write and build, so that nothing learned over twenty years is lost and every output starts from material already refined. · **Governs:** note-taking, the line between Notion and this repo, and how essays, chapters and papers get written. · **Carried out in Notion:** [Second Brain](https://app.notion.com/p/40bd55c9671b81b19d82cbaf0900f407)

## The flow: Capture, Organize, Distill, Express

1. **Capture** anything that resonates, in one place: a passage, an idea, a pattern from practice. Speed matters more than order here.
2. **Organize** by actionability, not by topic, using PARA:
   - **Projects:** an outcome with a deadline. These are the Gates and Tasks.
   - **Areas:** an ongoing responsibility. These are the stages and my four properties.
   - **Resources:** topics of interest, kept in Notes, Learning and this repo's `knowledge/`.
   - **Archives:** anything finished or set down.
3. **Distill** in layers, and only when a note is actually used:
   1. captured;
   2. key passages bolded;
   3. summarised in a line or two;
   4. rewritten in my own words.
4. **Express** by building outputs from **intermediate packets**: finished paragraphs, figures, arguments and reading lists. An essay assembled from packets takes days, not weeks.

## My twelve favourite problems

Every note is filed against at least one of these. A capture that touches none probably doesn't need keeping. The list is reviewed each November.

1. Does the accommodation process act as a disabling barrier in its own right, and through which design features?
2. What would an access process designed for lives that change look like?
3. How do we stop the documentation gate from deciding, by what people can afford, who gets accommodated?
4. How can access and mental health care meet at one door?
5. How does disability inclusion travel into global mental health systems and task-shifted care?
6. When does making help people heal, and how do we show it without reducing it?
7. How do I hold the person and the system at the same time?
8. What makes an institution actually change?
9. What makes an essential requirement defensible?
10. How do I do work of the highest quality by finishing fewer things?
11. Where is my effort most valuable: what is neglected that I'm unusually placed to do?
12. How do I build security and freedom together?

## Where knowledge lives

| Kind | Home |
|---|---|
| Raw captures, highlights, working notes | Notion *Notes* (private) |
| Notes in my own words that are still evolving | Notion *Notes*, at stages 3–4 |
| Evergreen principles, frameworks and findings | This repo, promoted from Notion |

**Promotion rule:** a stage-4 note that will stay true for years moves to the matching folder here (`knowledge/`, `philosophy/`, `facts/`), and Notion keeps the link.

## Guardrails

- **Patterns, never cases.** No student, client or colleague may be identifiable in any note.
- **Nothing in a programme application is written by AI** (`house-rules.md`). The Second Brain holds my own thinking; the application text is mine alone.
- **Capture freely, keep selectively.** The point is a smaller store that I trust, not a bigger one.
