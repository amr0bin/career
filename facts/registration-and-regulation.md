# Registration and regulation

> **Purpose:** what has to be true to practise in BC as a counsellor and later as a psychologist, and what must be chosen inside the MA for that to be true. · **Governs:** registration choices (RCC, CCC, CHCPBC, psychologist). · **Carried out in Notion:** {{notion:Calendar}}

---

## The ceiling

Two registrations and a protected title. RCC (and CCC) from the MA (~2034), RCAT from VATI (~2035), psychologist from the UBC PhD (~2041). The psychologist title brings diagnostic authority, and with it the documentation gate that decides who gets access.

## The landscape (verified 20 Sep 2026, chcpbc.org/legislation/regulating-psychotherapy)

| | Status |
|---|---|
| **HPOA** (Health Professions and Occupations Act) | In force 1 April 2026 |
| **Psychotherapy regulated** | **29 November 2027**, under the College of Health and Care Professionals of BC (CHCPBC), its tenth regulated profession |
| College applications | Open summer 2027 |
| Bylaw consultation | Autumn 2026; webinar 14 Oct 2026 |
| **Entry-to-practice standards** | **Not yet established**, in CHCPBC's own words |
| **Reserved title** | Not yet prescribed. CCPA and BCACC proposed "Clinical Counsellor" or "Psychotherapist" in 2023; 10-stability (6 Oct 2026) records the protected title as "psychotherapist" (**verify** which is final) |
| **Grandparenting** | "No designations will be automatically ported" (CCPA). A legacy route and a regular route for recent graduates are expected; criteria unpublished |

**What this means for a ~2034 graduate:** registration will be with CHCPBC under its regular route, whose requirements do not yet exist. Anyone who says otherwise is speculating.

## RCC vs CCC: the hedge is to clear both

They measure different things, so a programme that clears both has cleared more ground than either alone.

| | **RCC** (BCACC) | **CCC** (CCPA) |
|---|---|---|
| Coursework | 6 topic areas, including **group therapy** and **family therapy** | 8 named graduate courses, including practicum |
| Direct client hours | **None required** ("only clinical supervision hours") | **150** in supervised practicum |
| Supervision | **100 h, 25 direct** | **10 h direct** |
| Exam | None | None |
| Accredited fast track | — | CACEP graduates (UBC yes; SFU no) |
| After 2027 | Whether BCACC membership still matters is unresolved | The CCC trademark stays with CCPA; it does not guarantee college licensure |

**Plan to the higher of each:** 150+ direct hours; 100+ supervision hours with 25+ direct. A UBC Counselling Services practicum (600 h, at least 180 direct, about 2 h of individual supervision a week) clears both, which is why the practicum seat (~Jan 2033 application) is the hinge.

**The two decisions made inside the MA, written down now:** take **group therapy** theory and practice; take **family therapy** theory and practice. They are the usual failure points on an RCC application, and group facilitation is directly employable in both campus counselling services.

Sources: bcacc.ca/join/rcc/eligibility-rcc; ccpa-accp.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/CERTIFICATION-GUIDE_Nov2025.pdf; ccpa-accp.ca/british-columbia-regulation; ccpa-accp.ca/accreditation (all **verified** 20 Sep 2026).

## RCAT (after VATI)

Registered Canadian Art Therapist, through the Canadian Art Therapy Association, on completing the VATI Advanced Diploma. **verify** current requirements. Advertising and title rules for art therapy under the new psychotherapy regulation are also **verify**.

## Psychologist registration

| | |
|---|---|
| Requires | A doctorate in psychology. In BC the route runs through the UBC PhD in Counselling Psychology (CPA-accredited, 1,600-hour internship) |
| Body | CHCPBC (psychology) |
| Exams | EPPP and an oral exam (per education-path; **verify** current form at CHCPBC) |
| Confers | The protected title and diagnostic authority (B.C. Reg. 131/2025) |
| On the arc | PhD ~2035–40 → registration ~2041 (target) |

Sources: chcpbc.org/applicants/psychology; Regulated Health Practitioners Regulation, B.C. Reg. 131/2025 (**verified** 6 Oct 2026).

## Four things that could move, and how the plan survives each

| Could move | Task |
|---|---|
| Entry-to-practice standards are unwritten | Read the bylaw consultation; re-read all public pages when standards publish and again on 29 Nov 2027 |
| BCACC's role after regulation | Hold both RCC and CCC eligibility; decide sequencing in MA year 2 (`options/open-forks.md`) |
| The 29 Nov 2027 date slips (it has moved once before) | Nothing in the plan depends on it before ~2034 |
| Advertising standards for a property named *Therapeutic* Studio | Have the answer before 29 Nov 2027; not a reason to reopen the naming |

**Standing habits that make registration straightforward:** a supervision log (supervisor, date, duration, mode) from the crisis line onward, so the format is not invented in 2033. Crisis-line supervision is not clinical supervision for registration, but the habit is the same. (The log itself is private and never in this repo.)
