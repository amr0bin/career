## UBC MA Counselling Psychology (thesis) and SFU MA Counselling Psychology

### Courses: the union, which is what the ladder covers

⚑ **Prerequisite count, 6 to 9.** UBC lists 5 and SFU lists 6; whether skills and theories collapse into one course each is unknown until both equivalency tables are read. Best case 6 courses, worst case 9. data.js plans 6, career.html says 5, prerequisites.md says 6–9.

| Requirement | UBC | SFU | Planned course (primary) | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statistics / research methods | EPSE 482 or equivalent ("statistics") | Research methods (incl. statistics) | UBC EPSE 482 (400-level), Jan–Apr 2027 | Athabasca MATH 215; TRU-OL PSYC 2101 |
| Abnormal psychology | 3 credits (or behaviour disorders) | Counts as "one further" | UBC PSYC 300, May–Aug 2027 | Athabasca PSYC 435 |
| Learning / cognitive psychology | Learning, 3 credits (PSYC 309/304/363 accepted) | Cognitive or educational psychology | UBC PSYC 309, Jan–Apr 2028 | UBC PSYC 304; Athabasca PSYC 355 |
| Counselling theories | CNPS 365 or equivalent | Counselling theories (EDUC 323 exemplar) | SFU EDUC 323, May–Aug 2028 | UBC CNPS 365 online |
| Developmental psychology | — | Required | SFU EDUC 322 or 464, Sep–Dec 2028 | SFU PSYC 250 |
| Interviewing / helping skills | CNPS 362 or closest equivalent | Counselling skills (EDUC 423 exemplar) | SFU EDUC 423, 2029 | Douglas PSYC 4370; KPU CNPS 4300 |

All graded by **31 Dec 2030**. Course terms come from education-path (planned for a 2029 deadline), which leaves about a year of slack against the 2030 deadline.

## RCC (BCACC) and CCC (CCPA): built inside the MA

| Requirement | RCC | CCC | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Degree | Master's in counselling or a relevant area | Graduate counselling degree | MA conferred |
| Coursework | 6 areas: developmental or psychopathology; counselling or personality theories; **group therapy**; **family therapy**; research design; ethics | 8 standalone graduate courses: theories, skills, practicum, ethics + 4 electives from designated areas (max 2 per category) | Transcript; **group and family therapy taken as electives** |
| Supervision | 100 h, at least 25 direct | 10 h direct minimum | A supervision log in a registrar-legible format |
| Direct client hours | None required | 150 minimum (75% relational) | Practicum log; UBC Counselling Services practicum gives ~180 direct |
| Other | 2 references on BCACC forms; Canadian residency; $200 fee; no exam | No exam; CACEP graduates fast-tracked | — |

**Plan to the higher of both:** 150+ direct client hours and 100+ supervision hours with 25+ direct.
Sources: bcacc.ca/join/rcc/eligibility-rcc; CCPA Certification Guide Nov 2025 (ccpa-accp.ca).
