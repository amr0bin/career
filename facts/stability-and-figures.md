## Three findings

1. **Security arrives at Stage 1, not at the end.** The first role on this path is already a six-figure, pensioned career; everything after it adds range and choice.
2. **Every later stage has funding that can be named:** a tuition waiver, a $27,000 master's scholarship, and a $40,000-a-year doctoral minimum at UBC.
3. **The psychologist title in BC runs through one door**, the UBC PhD in Counselling Psychology, which admits only from a thesis MA. This confirms the thesis MA as load-bearing.

## Seven enablers, in order of leverage

1. **Get inside a university as a continuing employee.** One move delivers the salary band, pension, waiver, internal-candidate route and committee seat. A term role is the way in; conversion is the target.
2. **Choose the employer with Stage 2 in mind.** SFU: MA tuition waived, evening classes. UBC: prerequisites free, partial MA waiver, the accredited doctorate. Price the difference at offer.
3. **Treat prerequisite courses as scholarship evidence.** Master's and doctoral awards are adjudicated on academic record and research potential.
4. **Start the helping-experience clock now.** Crisis Centre BC is the clock (UBC's figure is unresolved; see `facts/verification-register.md`).
5. **Have a supervisor who wants the question before applying.** SFU admits at most 12 from 250 to 300 applicants.
6. **Publish before each gate.**
7. **Build one asset that earns without her presence.**
